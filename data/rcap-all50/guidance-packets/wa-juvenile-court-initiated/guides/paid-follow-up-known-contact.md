# Washington court-initiated juvenile sealing guide

This guide helps you check the court's action under RCW 13.50.260(1)-(2). It does not confirm that your record is sealed.

Court: SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — juvenile court identified in participant's case record

Case number: SYNTHETIC-CASE-001

Court contact: Use your court's official directory

Juvenile department contact: SYNTHETIC Juvenile Department — 555-0100 Records desk

## Your next step

Contact the juvenile department named in the denial, provide actual payment proof for the remaining individual-victim restitution and request administrative sealing follow-up. The department verifies payment and circulates the order for signature; the clerk seals after receiving the signed order.

## Information to obtain before taking that step

- The court clerk's current contact details, from that court's official directory.

## Keep a record of the response

Record when you contacted the court or juvenile department, whom you contacted and what they said. Obtain the actual written order and check which case and records it covers. A missing public search result alone does not establish sealing.

This guide starts no participant court filing and quotes no filing fee or fee waiver. Ask the relevant office about any copy or record-request charge. The separate subsection (3) motion and its notice requirements are not supplied by this guide.

For contested eligibility, a disputed restitution amount, an appeal or a denial outside the payment follow-up described here, take the actual records to a legal-aid professional. Do not sign for a court, certify notice or assume a judge has ruled.
