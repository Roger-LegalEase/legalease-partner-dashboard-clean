# Veterans treatment court participation and completion

Taking part in veterans treatment court and completing the program come before this filing. This packet does not create program participation and does not prove completion.

This packet does not describe local veterans treatment court admission rules or what completion requires. Ask the program or the court that handled the case.

The statute behind this kind of sealing is still being confirmed, so this packet does not say who qualifies. Do not treat it as an answer about your eligibility.

## What you will be asked

- Have you completed veterans treatment court, and on what date?
- Which court and county handled the case, and what was the charge?

## What this step does not do

- It does not tell you whether you qualify.
- It does not create, replace, or stand in for any court form.
- It does not contact the court, the prosecuting attorney, or any program on your behalf.

## Stop and get a lawyer if

- The prosecuting attorney files an objection.
- The court sets a contested hearing.
- Immigration, licensing or firearm consequences are in play.
- You have not finished the program.
