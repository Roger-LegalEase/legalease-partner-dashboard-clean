# Act 346 first offender placement

Act 346 is chosen early — at the plea stage, not afterward. It is the strongest outcome available in an Arkansas case: the charge is dismissed and sealed, with no conviction. Because it is elected at the plea, it is not something you can add later.

This packet does not create or recreate that plea election. It prepares the later filing, after you confirm that you were placed on probation as a first offender under Act 346 and that you completed the probation.

The prosecuting attorney may have to agree at the plea stage. That is a negotiation, and this packet cannot do it for you.

This packet does not describe local program intake rules. If you are not sure whether Act 346 applied to your case, check your court paperwork or ask the court that handled the case.

## What you will be asked

- Were you placed on probation under Act 346 as a first offender?
- Have you completed the probation, and on what date?
- Which court and county handled the case, and what was the charge?

## What this step does not do

- It does not tell you whether you qualify.
- It does not create, replace, or stand in for any court form.
- It does not contact the court, the prosecuting attorney, or any program on your behalf.

## Stop and get a lawyer if

- The prosecuting attorney files an objection.
- The court sets a contested hearing.
- Immigration, licensing or firearm consequences are in play.
- You are still on your Act 346 probation.
- Your case is still at the plea stage and needs the prosecuting attorney to agree — that is a negotiation, and this packet cannot do it for you.
