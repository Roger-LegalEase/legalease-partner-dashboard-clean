# Act 346 First Offender Dismiss And Seal — what you do

This packet does not include the official Arkansas forms for this filing. Arkansas uses standardized AOC / ACIC uniform petition-and-order to seal forms, posted on the Department of Public Safety / ACIC site. Get the current petition-and-order pair for your situation from the Arkansas Crime Information Center before you start filling anything in:
https://dps.arkansas.gov/crime-info-support/arkansas-crime-information-center/forms/criminal-history/

## Before you file

- Get a fingerprint card. Have fingerprints taken and submit the card with the petition. Fingerprint cards are yours to obtain — from a law enforcement agency or an authorised fingerprint vendor.
- Get your Arkansas criminal history. Submit the ACIC Authorization for Review of Criminal History Information to obtain your record. Confirming the record fixes the offense class, the disposition, and the completion dates.
- Check your answers against that record and correct the packet if they disagree.

## Steps

1. Get the record. Pull the ACIC criminal history and the court docket / Judgment and Commitment Order to confirm each charge, the offense class, the disposition, and the sentence-completion dates.
2. Confirm completion. Verify all incarceration, probation, fines, restitution, court costs, and license reinstatement are satisfied — this is the usual practical gate.
3. Complete the uniform petition and order for the matching disposition, citing the statute, case number, offense, and class.
4. Sign and date the petition where the form calls for your signature. You sign your own petition.
5. File in the circuit or district court that handled the case. Act 1460 eliminated filing fees for sealing.
6. Serve the prosecuting attorney within three days of filing. The prosecuting attorney has 30 days to object. (The general Arkansas filing instructions state 30 days for a misdemeanor and 90 days for a felony — ask the clerk which applies to your case.)
7. Ruling and distribution. If granted, the court enters the uniform order; the clerk sends copies to ACIC, the arresting agency, the prosecutor, and the Administrative Office of the Courts, which update their repositories to seal the record.

## What is left blank for you

- The court and county are not filled in. File in the court that handled your case. If you have cases in more than one court, you file in each.
- The case number, the judge, and the prosecuting attorney's name are not filled in. Take these from your own court paperwork or from your criminal history.
- Your date of birth and identifying numbers are not filled in.
- Nothing in this packet says what the prosecuting attorney will do, whether you will get a hearing, or how the court will rule.

## Costs

Act 1460 eliminated filing fees for record sealing in Arkansas. We do not have that confirmed for this particular petition, so ask the clerk of the court that handled your case what, if anything, you owe before you file. There is a separate ACIC fee for the criminal-history record.

## Stop and get a lawyer if

- The prosecuting attorney files an objection.
- The court sets a contested hearing.
- Immigration, licensing or firearm consequences are in play.
- You are still on your Act 346 probation.
- Your case is still at the plea stage and needs the prosecuting attorney to agree — that is a negotiation, and this packet cannot do it for you.
