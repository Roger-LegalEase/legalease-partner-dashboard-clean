# Drug court admission and completion

Admission to drug court and completion of the program come before this filing. This packet does not create program admission and does not prove completion.

Getting into drug court can require the prosecuting attorney to agree. That happens before this filing, it is a negotiation, and this packet cannot do it for you.

Which petition-and-order pair applies depends on whether you were in drug court before adjudication or after. You will be asked that question, and the answer decides which official pair belongs in the filing.

This packet does not describe local drug court admission rules or what completion requires. Ask the drug court program or the court that handled the case.

## What you will be asked

- Have you completed drug court, and on what date?
- Were you in drug court before adjudication, or after?
- Which court and county handled the case, and what was the charge?

## What this step does not do

- It does not tell you whether you qualify.
- It does not create, replace, or stand in for any court form.
- It does not contact the court, the prosecuting attorney, or any program on your behalf.

## Stop and get a lawyer if

- The prosecuting attorney files an objection.
- The court sets a contested hearing.
- Immigration, licensing or firearm consequences are in play.
- You have not finished the program.
- Getting into the program needs the prosecuting attorney to agree — that is a negotiation, and this packet cannot do it for you.
