# Records after a nolle

Connecticut calls this erasure. Not expungement, not sealing.

## If the charge was already nolled — there is nothing to file

The law erases the records thirteen months after the nolle. No form, no fee, no filing.

**What to do instead:**

1. Look the case up on the Connecticut Judicial Branch online case look-up and confirm the date the charge was nolled. You obtain this yourself; LegalEase does not obtain, receive or inspect it.
2. Count thirteen months from that date yourself.
3. After that, check the look-up again. **An erased case will not appear.** If the case is gone, that is your answer.
4. If your case is still showing after the thirteen months are up, the record was not erased when it should have been. That is a different path — an agency process for fixing a missed erasure, not a court filing.

If what the look-up shows disagrees with the answers you gave, correct the packet.

## If the charge is still being continued at the prosecutor's request

Where a charge was continued at the prosecutor's request and thirteen months have gone by with no prosecution and no other disposition, the law says the charge **shall be nolled** on your motion. Once it is nolled, the erasure rules above apply.

**This packet cannot prepare that motion.** No form for it was found, and the caption, the exact filing location, whether anything must be served and what clerks accept in practice have not been settled. Take this one to a lawyer or a legal-aid clinic.

## If the nolle was entered before 1 April 1972

Those records are already deemed erased by operation of law in the older courts the statute lists, and the arrested person or an heir may petition to have them erased. Nothing further about that petition is settled here, so this packet does not prepare it either.

## Fees

No court fee is charged in any court for a petition under this statute.

## Signing, notarizing, service

- **Signing.** Nothing to sign on the automatic path. Unresolved on the motion, because it depends on the vehicle.
- **Notarizing.** The sources do not state a requirement.
- **Service.** Unresolved on the motion. No service requirement was established.

## Stop and get a lawyer if

- You need the motion for a charge that is still being continued.
- The thirteen months have not yet gone by.
- Your record was not erased when it should have been.
- You have an immigration matter.
