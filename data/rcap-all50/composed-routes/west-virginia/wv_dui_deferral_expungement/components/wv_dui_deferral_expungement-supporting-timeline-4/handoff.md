# Handoff — Supporting timeline (§ 17C-5-2b(g)(1))

Stage-two supporting document. See the route-level `handoff.md` for the full picture.

- **Why it exists**: the registry packetSet declares `supporting_timeline` a required custom-pleading
  component, and the controlling review records that what can be prepared at this stage is "the application
  and the supporting timeline". The one-year period runs from the expiration of probation and not from the
  dismissal, and the two dates are usually different.
- **The only unit whose `participantTreatment.prefill` is `confirm` rather than `screening`**: every date
  on it is an answer the registry requires to be checked against a participant-obtained document before
  filing.
- **Computes nothing.** No interval is calculated, no conclusion is drawn that the period has run, and no
  eligibility is asserted. The negative fixture fails specifically on computing the period — and on
  computing it from the dismissal date.
- **Boundary fixture** covers the participant who has three of the four documents but not the written
  confirmation of the probation-expiry date: the answer renders as the participant's own and unconfirmed,
  and the dismissal date is never allowed to stand in for it.
