# The application asking the court to clear the record

This is the document that asks the court to clear the record of the arrest, trial and conviction, now that
your case has been dismissed and at least a year has run since your probation ended.

Everything in it is your own answer. Read it through and correct anything that is wrong.

## The date that matters

The one-year wait runs from **the date your probation term expired**, not from the date your case was
dismissed. Those are usually different dates. Get written confirmation of the probation expiry date from
the probation office or the clerk before you file, and check the date in this application against it.

This application states your dates. It does not work out for you whether the year has run — that is for
the court.

## Before you sign it

You need four things in hand: the Division of Motor Vehicles certification that you completed the program,
a certified copy of the order that deferred your case, a certified copy of the order dismissing and
discharging you, and written confirmation of the date your probation expired.

## What to do

1. Fill in the court, the county and the case number in the caption.
2. Leave the hearing date blank. A hearing only happens if somebody objects, and the court sets the date if
   it does.
3. Sign and date the application. This one does not have to be notarised.
4. File it with the clerk of the same court, together with the timeline that goes with it.
5. Send a copy to the prosecuting attorney and file the certificate of service that says you did.

## What happens after

Objections are filed within 30 days after you serve the prosecuting attorney. If objections are filed, the
court holds a hearing where everyone gets to be heard on whether you broke the conditions of your probation
seriously or repeatedly. If the court decides you did not, it orders the record cleared.

**If somebody objects and the court sets a hearing, get a lawyer.** That is where help of this kind ends.

## What the order does not reach

Your driving record. The law says in terms that the records the Division of Motor Vehicles keeps are not
cleared by an order made under this section, and this application does not ask for them. If your driving
record is what you need, this is not it.

Stop and talk to a lawyer if you have ever been convicted of a felony, anywhere. That blocks this request
outright.

## What is left blank for you

- The court, the county and the case number. Take them from your own court paperwork. If you are not sure
  how that court styles a caption, ask the clerk before you file.
- Your signature.
- Your date of birth and any identifying numbers. Nothing here asks for them.

Nothing in this document says how the court will rule, or when. Nothing in it speaks for the prosecuting
attorney.
