# The sworn statement that goes with the motion

The law says the motion to dismiss has to be supported by your own sworn statement. This is it.

Everything in it is a fact you know yourself: when the court deferred your case, how long your probation
was, when you finished the Motor Vehicle Alcohol Test and Lock Program, and that you kept to the conditions
of your probation. Read it through and correct anything that is wrong before you swear to it.

It does not say anything about what the Division of Motor Vehicles certification contains. That is for the
certification itself, which you attach separately.

## What to do

1. Fill in the court, the county and the case number in the caption.
2. **Do not sign it in advance.** This one has to be sworn in front of a notary public or another official
   who can take an affidavit. Take it to them unsigned.
3. Sign and date it in front of them, and let them complete their part at the foot of the document.
4. File it with the motion.

## One thing we could not settle

We do not have a source that tells us the exact wording your court expects in the notary's part of this
document, or which officials in West Virginia can take it. That part is left open rather than filled in
with a guess. If your notary or the clerk wants different wording, use theirs.

## Do not swear to anything you are not sure of

If any date in this statement is one you are working from memory, get the document that proves it before
you swear to it. If anyone has ever said you broke the conditions of your probation, stop and talk to a
lawyer before signing anything.

## What is left blank for you

- The court, the county and the case number. Take them from your own court paperwork. If you are not sure
  how that court styles a caption, ask the clerk before you file.
- Your signature.
- Your date of birth and any identifying numbers. Nothing here asks for them.

Nothing in this document says how the court will rule, or when. Nothing in it speaks for the prosecuting
attorney.
