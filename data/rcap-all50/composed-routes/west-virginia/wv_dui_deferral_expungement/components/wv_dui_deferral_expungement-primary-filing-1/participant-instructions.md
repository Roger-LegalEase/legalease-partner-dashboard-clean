# The motion asking the court to dismiss the charges

This is the document that asks the court to dismiss the charges against you, now that you have completed
the Motor Vehicle Alcohol Test and Lock Program and kept to the conditions of your probation.

Everything in it is your own answer. Read it through and correct anything that is wrong.

## Before you sign it

You need two things in hand:

1. The Division of Motor Vehicles certification that you successfully completed the test and lock program.
   The law requires this motion to be supported by it, so it goes in with the motion. Ask the Division for
   it — nobody obtains it for you.
2. A certified copy of the order that deferred your case and put you on probation. Check the dates in this
   motion against it and correct them if they disagree.

## What to do

1. Fill in the court, the county and the case number in the caption.
2. Sign and date the motion.
3. File it with the clerk of the court that deferred your case, together with the sworn statement that
   goes with it and the Division of Motor Vehicles certification.
4. Send a copy to the prosecuting attorney and file the certificate of service that says you did.

## What happens after

The prosecuting attorney has 30 days from the day you serve them to tell the judge about any objections.
If none are filed in that time, the court dismisses the charges. Nobody here can tell you whether an
objection will be filed or what the court will do.

## What this document does not do

This motion asks for the charges to be dismissed. It does not ask for your record to be cleared — that is
a separate request you cannot make until at least a year after your probation term expires. This document
says nothing about it, and nothing here promises it.

It also does not touch your driving record. Nothing in this route ever does.

## What is left blank for you

- The court, the county and the case number. Take them from your own court paperwork. If you are not sure
  how that court styles a caption, ask the clerk before you file.
- Your signature.
- Your date of birth and any identifying numbers. Nothing here asks for them.

Nothing in this document says how the court will rule, or when. Nothing in it speaks for the prosecuting
attorney.

This is legal information, not legal advice. It does not guarantee eligibility or any court outcome.
