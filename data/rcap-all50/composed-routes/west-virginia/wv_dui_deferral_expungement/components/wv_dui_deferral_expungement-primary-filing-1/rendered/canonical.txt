Code § 17C-5-2b(c) — When the defendant has satisfactorily completed the Motor Vehicle Alcohol Test and Lock Program and complied with its conditions, the defendant may move the court for an order dismissing the charges; the motion must be supported by the defendant's affidavit and by certification of the Division of Motor Vehicles that the programme was successfully completed; the prosecuting attorney has 30 days after service to advise the judge of objections, in the absence of which the court shall dismiss.
