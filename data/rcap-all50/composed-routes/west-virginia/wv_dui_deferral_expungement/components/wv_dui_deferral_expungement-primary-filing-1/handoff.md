# Handoff — Motion to dismiss (§ 17C-5-2b(c))

Stage-one primary filing. See the route-level `handoff.md` for authority, mechanism, route decision and
the full counsel-flag list; this note carries only what is specific to this document.

- **Authority**: § 17C-5-2b(a),(b) (deferral predicate), (c) (the motion), (d) (effect), (e),(f) (limits).
- **Mechanism**: on satisfactory completion of the test and lock program and compliance with the conditions
  of probation, the defendant may move the court for an order dismissing the charges. The motion must be
  supported by the defendant's affidavit and by DMV certification of successful completion. The prosecuting
  attorney has 30 days after service to advise the judge of objections; absent objections the court shall
  dismiss.
- **Route decision**: custom pleading. No West Virginia Judiciary form exists for a § 17C-5-2b filing —
  the Court Forms index publishes five expungement forms, all § 61-11-25 or § 61-11-26 instruments.
- **Vocabulary**: `expunge` / `expunged` / `expunging` / `expungement` are prohibited here in addition to
  the route-wide list. This stage produces dismissal and discharge; expungement is a separate submission.
- **Open flags specific to this document**: `wv-dui-deferral-stale-file-vehicle` (release blocker — whether
  the motion is still the right vehicle on an administratively closed file is not established, and nothing
  in the document asserts that it is); `wv-dui-deferral-felony-bar-not-in-controlling-review` (a prior
  felony conviction does NOT exclude this document — the bar sits in subsection (g), and that reading is an
  open counsel question).
- **F-review**: verification is absent by design — subsection (c) requires the motion to be *supported by*
  an affidavit, not to be verified itself. `verificationStatute.citation` is null and no block renders.
