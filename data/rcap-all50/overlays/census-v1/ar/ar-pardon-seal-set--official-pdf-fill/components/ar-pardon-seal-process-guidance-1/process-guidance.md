# Getting the pardon

A pardon has to be granted before this sealing filing is available. This packet does not prepare a pardon application.

You will need the pardon documentation from the granting authority. Attach it to the petition; this packet does not review it.

This packet does not describe how to apply for a pardon. Get that process from the authority that grants pardons.

If a pardon has not been granted yet, stop here — the sealing filing is not available to you yet.

## What you will be asked

- Has a pardon been granted, and on what date?
- What was the conviction — which court, county, offence and statute section?
- Was this a youthful felony offender adjudication?

## What this step does not do

- It does not tell you whether you qualify.
- It does not create, replace, or stand in for any court form.
- It does not contact the court, the prosecuting attorney, or any program on your behalf.

## Stop and get a lawyer if

- The prosecuting attorney files an objection.
- The court sets a contested hearing.
- Immigration, licensing or firearm consequences are in play.
- You have not been granted a pardon yet.
