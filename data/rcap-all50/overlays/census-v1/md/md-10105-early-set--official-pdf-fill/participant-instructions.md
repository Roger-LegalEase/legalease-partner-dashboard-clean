# What to do with this packet - Ask the court to expunge now instead of waiting three years

This packet is two Maryland Judiciary forms:

- **CC-DC-CR-072C**, _Petition for Expungement of Records - Acquittal, Dismissal, Not Guilty, or Nolle Prosequi_, for a case where **less than three years** has passed since the disposition. This is the form you file. Its lower half is the **General Waiver and Release (Form 4-503.2)** - the waiver is printed on the same sheet as the petition.
- **CC-DC-CR-078**, the standalone _General Waiver and Release (Form 4-503.2)_. The committed component record marks this one **conditional**, and states the condition in these words: "Where the alternative route is used: CC-DC-CR-072A plus a standalone waiver." **If you are filing CC-DC-CR-072C, you do not also sign this sheet** - the waiver you are signing is already on the petition. It is included so that a filer on the alternative route has it.

Both are prepared for one route - **Petition for Expungement with General Waiver and Release under § 10-105(c)(1)** - under Md. Code, Crim. Proc. § 10-105(c)(1); Md. Rule 4-503.2.

## Read this before you file anything: what the waiver costs you

The committed legal-design record for this route carries this instruction to anyone who offers it, and this page exists to comply with it:

> Whenever this route is offered, tell the participant that waiting costs nothing and preserves their tort claim, and that filing now trades that claim for speed. Presenting this route without that comparison is a defect.

In plain terms: this route is fast **because** you sign away something. Section 10-105(c)(1) removes the three-year wait only where a written general waiver and release of all tort claims arising from the charge is filed with the petition. Signing it gives up your right to sue the arresting agency, its officers, its agents and its employees over this arrest, detention or confinement. **Waiting three years costs you nothing and keeps that right.**

## There may be nothing for you to file at all

The same record says, in its own words:

> Where all charges ended in acquittal, dismissal, not guilty or nolle prosequi and the disposition was entered on or after October 1, 2021, the case expunges automatically at three years under § 10-105.1. Surface the automatic route before offering this one.

So check that first. If every charge in your case ended in one of those four ways and the disposition was entered on or after 1 October 2021, waiting is free and automatic, and this packet is not the route you want.

## What this route covers, and what it does not

Where a written general waiver and release of all tort claims arising from the charge is filed with the petition, the three-year wait does not apply. The eligible disposition set is narrower than the ordinary petition and does not include PBJ or stet.

The dispositions this form reaches, as the form prints them:

- you were acquitted of the charge(s);
- the charge(s) was otherwise dismissed;
- you were found not guilty;
- a nolle prosequi was entered **without** a requirement of drug or alcohol treatment.

What is outside it:

- Probation before judgment and stet are outside this route; the form's checkbox list is narrower than CC-DC-CR-072A's.
- A nolle prosequi entered with a drug or alcohol treatment requirement.
- The unit rule under § 10-107.

**Every charge in the incident matters, not only the one you want cleared.** The record this packet is built from puts it this way:

> Ask about every charge in the incident. The unit rule at § 10-107(b)(1) means one ineligible charge in a unit can defeat the whole unit, subject to the § 10-107(a) minor-traffic and cannabis-possession carve-outs. Whether ambiguous charges form a unit is a legal judgment and is not automated.

## What the platform filled in, and what it did not

The platform wrote what it holds about you and your case: your name, your date of birth, your street address, your city, state and zip, and the case number - on both forms - and your telephone number and e-mail on the petition. Everything else is yours, and every one of those blanks is listed below by the form and the section it is in. Nothing was guessed.

**Your telephone number is on the petition and not on CC-DC-CR-078.** The petition prints one unqualified _Telephone_ line and that is where your number went. CC-DC-CR-078 asks for a **home** number and a **work** number in separate boxes; the platform holds one number and no fact about which kind it is, so both boxes are left for you.

## What you must do before you file

1. **Look the case up on Maryland Judiciary Case Search** and confirm the court, the case number, every charge and the disposition. It is free. If anything on the packet disagrees with the case record, correct the packet.
2. **Fill in every item in the tables below.** Each names the form, the section and the blank.
3. **Make the choices listed under _The choices that are yours_.** Which court, which city or county, how you were charged, and how the charge ended.
4. **List every charge in the incident** in paragraph 2, not only the one you want cleared.
5. **Read paragraphs 1 to 5 and the affirmation above your signature.** By signing you affirm under penalties of perjury that the contents are true, that the charge was not a nonincarcerable vehicle or traffic matter, and that it is not part of a unit whose expungement is precluded under Crim. Proc. § 10-107.
6. **Sign the petition yourself, and sign the waiver in front of a witness** who prints and signs their own name. Date both on the day you sign.
7. **Leave the attorney column alone** - it is the left-hand column of the CC-DC-CR-072C signature block - unless a lawyer is filing for you.

## Where it goes, what it costs, and who is told

Each line below is the committed record's own words for this route.

- **Where:** File with the clerk of the court that heard the case. Clerk of the District Court or circuit court that heard the case. In person, by mail, or MDEC e-filing where the county is on MDEC. The court serves the State's Attorney under § 10-105(d)(1).
- **The fee, as the committed record states it:** $0.
- **Fee waiver:** Not applicable. There is no fee.
- **Who serves the State's Attorney:** The court serves. The State's Attorney is served by the court; 30-day objection period.
- **Notarization:** none.
- **Your signature:** Required on both the petition and the waiver.
- **Service by you:** The court serves the State's Attorney. The participant does not serve anyone and no certificate of service is filed.
- **A proposed order:** Do not generate a proposed order. The Maryland court generates the order. That is why this packet contains none.

## When this is not a do-it-yourself matter

The record names these as the points where this route stops being a self-help matter. Stop and get a lawyer if any is true:

- The participant is unsure whether to trade the tort claim for speed.
- The State's Attorney objects.
- Any indication of a viable claim against the arresting agency.

A timely State's Attorney objection converts the matter into a contested hearing. Generate the initial packet; hand off when an objection is actually filed.

## After an expungement is granted

One thing the record insists is surfaced, in its own words:

> Tell the participant that although § 10-109 bars an employer, educational institution or State or local unit from requiring disclosure of expunged charges, the Judiciary's own brochure warns a person may still be required to disclose expunged cases in situations Maryland law does not govern.

## What this packet is not

This is a prepared copy of two official Maryland Judiciary forms. It is not legal advice, it is not filed for you, and it does not decide whether your case can be expunged or whether signing the waiver is the right trade for you to make. Read the form against your own record before you sign it.
