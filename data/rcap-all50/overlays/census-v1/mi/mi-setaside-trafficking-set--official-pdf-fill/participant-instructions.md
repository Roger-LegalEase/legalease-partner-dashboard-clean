# Filing instructions — Set aside convictions directly resulting from human trafficking

This packet is SCAO form **MC 227b**, prepared under MCL 780.621(3), MCL 780.621d; SCAO form MC 227b (Rev. 7/24). It is for convictions you contend were a direct result of being a victim of human trafficking. Use MC 227a for the separate misdemeanor-marijuana route and MC 227 for other set-aside applications.

## Before filing

1. Get a certified copy of each conviction from the clerk of the convicting court. Compare the exact charge, code, conviction date, and case number with every populated conviction row and correct any disagreement.
2. Complete every remaining field listed below. In particular, write your own account in item 2 of how the convictions directly resulted from human trafficking. LegalEase does not invent, assess, or shorten that statement.
3. Attach each certified conviction record. Use a separate application for each court, and make five copies of the application and attachments.
4. Swear to the application and sign it only in the presence of a deputy court clerk or notary. Do not prefill the signature, sworn date, or notarial certificate.
5. Obtain a fingerprint card from local law enforcement. A fingerprinting fee may apply.
6. Prepare a $50 check or money order payable to the State of Michigan for the Michigan State Police. The committed record does not establish a waiver of that statutory processing fee; stop and ask the convicting-court clerk or counsel if you cannot pay it.

## File and serve

File in the court where the conviction occurred. Depending on local practice, the clerk may set the hearing and complete the Notice of Hearing. Mail the application packet, fingerprint card, and $50 fee to Michigan State Police, Criminal Justice Information Center — Criminal History, PO Box 30266, Lansing, MI 48909. Mail a copy to the Michigan Attorney General, Assistance with Convictions and Expungements Division, PO Box 30212, Lansing, MI 48909, and mail a copy to the prosecuting official for the conviction.

Complete the Proof of Service on page 3 only after those mailings. Its dates, selections, and signature are intentionally blank in the generated packet because they certify acts that have not yet happened.

## Stop and get legal help

Stop automated assistance if the prosecution disputes the trafficking connection, the court sets a contested hearing, the records disagree, another application is pending or was denied, immigration consequences are possible, or individualized advocacy is needed.
