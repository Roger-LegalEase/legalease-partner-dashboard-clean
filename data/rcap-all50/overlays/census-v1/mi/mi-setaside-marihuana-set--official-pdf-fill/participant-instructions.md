# Filing instructions — Set aside a misdemeanour marijuana conviction

This packet is SCAO form **MC 227a**, _Application to Set Aside Misdemeanor Marihuana Conviction(s)_, prepared under MCL 780.621e; MCL 780.621b, MCL 780.621f, MCL 780.622, MCL 780.623; SCAO form MC 227a (Rev. 7/24).

The platform filled in what it holds: your name, your address and your telephone number in the defendant's contact box, and the county and the case number in the caption. Everything else is yours, and every one of those blanks is listed below by the part of the form it is in. Page 2 of your packet is the court's own instruction sheet — read it; the steps below follow it.

## Check you are on the right form

MC 227a is for **misdemeanour marijuana convictions**, which MCL 780.621e(7) defines and the court's instruction sheet lists: MCL 333.7403(2)(d) (possession), MCL 333.7404(2)(d) (use), MCL 333.7453 (selling marijuana paraphernalia), and local ordinances substantially corresponding to those.

The form itself routes two other applications away, in its own words:

- If the offence was **a direct result of your being a victim of human trafficking**, and you are applying under MCL 780.621(3), use **MC 227b**.
- If the conviction is **a non-marijuana misdemeanour** under MCL 780.621, use **MC 227**.

## Where you file, and the certified copies

**File in the court where the conviction happened**, and use a **separate application for each court** — instruction 2. **There should be no filing fee**, instruction 6 says so. But instruction 4 says to get a **certified copy of each conviction** from the clerk of the convicting court and attach it, and warns there may be a fee for those copies. The platform does not obtain them.

## The order of the last three steps matters

The court's instruction sheet is specific, and the certificate of mailing at the foot of page 1 is sworn:

1. **Sign and date the application** (instruction 5).
2. **Make three copies** of the application and every attachment, and take them to the clerk of the convicting court (instruction 6).
3. **Mail a copy of the whole packet to the prosecuting official** who prosecuted the offence (instruction 7).
4. **Only then fill in and sign the Certificate of Mailing** on both remaining copies, and take or mail one to the court (instruction 8).

The certificate is a declaration under the penalties of perjury that you have already mailed the packet. Its date and its signature are left blank in your copy for that reason, and nothing should be written on them until step 4.

## What you must do before you file

1. **Fill in every item in the table below.**
2. **Complete the conviction table.** Every line you use, all four columns — instruction 4 tells you to get the exact date and charge from the court.
3. **Make the choices listed under _The choices that are yours_.**
4. **Follow the four steps above, in that order.**

## What the platform deliberately left blank

- **Your signature and its date.**
- **The whole Certificate of Mailing.** It is sworn, and it certifies something that has not happened when this packet is prepared.
- **The judge's name.** The court assigns it.
- **The attorney block.** You are applying yourself; no attorney-representation fact is held for you.

## What a set-aside does not reach

An order setting aside a conviction is not a clean slate everywhere. The adopted record requires this packet to state plainly what the relief does not reach:

- **Your Secretary of State driving record survives.** A set-aside does not clear it.
- **SORA registration and reporting obligations continue** for a listed offence, per the note on MC 228.
- **Firearm rights are not restored.**
- **Restitution obligations survive.**
- **No fine, costs or other money paid is returned.** You are not entitled to get any of it back.

Restitution obligations survive. The applicant is not entitled to return of any fine, costs or other money paid.

## What happens after you file

The court's instruction sheet says: if the prosecuting agency files a response opposing your application, the court must set a hearing within 30 days and mail you notice of it, and you should appear. If no answer is filed within 60 days of service, the court enters an order and mails a copy to you, the arresting agency, the prosecuting agency and the Michigan State Police.

## What this packet is not

This is a prepared copy of an official SCAO form. It is not legal advice, it is not filed for you, and it does not decide whether your conviction is eligible to be set aside.
