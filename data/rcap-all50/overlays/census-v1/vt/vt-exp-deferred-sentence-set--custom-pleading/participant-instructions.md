# What you must do — confirming that a completed Vermont deferred sentence was expunged as 13 V.S.A. § 7041(e) requires, and asking the court in writing where the order did not issue

This packet is prepared for **Expungement on Discharge from a Deferred Sentence, 13 V.S.A. § 7041(e)**.

**You do not petition for this relief — the court owes it.** On discharge from probation after a fulfilled deferred sentence, 13 V.S.A. § 7041(e) requires the record expunged absent a finding of good cause, with no fee of any kind. The first work is to confirm three facts, and only where all three pass and the record still shows does the written request apply.

## The three checks, in order

| Check | Where |
| --- | --- |
| You were **discharged from probation** and the **adjudication of guilt was struck** — get the discharge order | the clerk of the Criminal Division of the Superior Court that entered the deferred sentence |
| **Restitution is paid in full** — the record is not expunged until it is, however long that takes | the Restitution Unit of the Vermont Center for Crime Victim Services (current balance statement) |
| Whether the **expungement order issued** — an expunged case will not show | Form 200-00331, Request for Criminal Record Search, submitted to the Criminal Division in the county |

How long courts take in practice to enter the order after discharge is not established by any source this packet is built from — before concluding the order is missing rather than pending, ask the Criminal Division clerk how long entry typically takes.

## The items you must supply (request stage only)

Each is printed on the request as a labelled dotted blank.

### vt_exp_deferred_sentence-written-request-to-court-2 — Written Request for Entry of the Expungement Order Under 13 V.S.A. Sec. 7041(e)

| The blank on the document | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Date of discharge from probation, with the adjudication of guilt struck, from the discharge order | the date you were discharged from probation and the adjudication of guilt was struck - copy it from the discharge order the Criminal Division clerk gives you; it establishes the date the Sec. 7041(e) duty attached |
| Docket number of the deferred-sentence case | the docket number of the deferred-sentence case, copied from your papers or from the discharge order |
| Date restitution was satisfied, from the Restitution Unit's statement | the date all restitution was paid in full, from the current balance statement of the Restitution Unit of the Vermont Center for Crime Victim Services - the record cannot be expunged until restitution is paid in full, so do not send the request without this |
| County written into the address block - the county whose Criminal Division entered the deferred sentence | the county whose Criminal Division of the Superior Court entered the deferred sentence - the request goes back to that same court, under the existing docket |

## What you do, in order (request stage)

1. **Run the three checks above.** If the case has cleared, you are done. If you were not discharged or restitution is outstanding, the request must not be sent.
2. **Fill in every dotted blank** from the discharge order and the Restitution Unit statement. Do not guess a date.
3. **Sign and date the request yourself.** Notarization is not required.
4. **Send it to the Criminal Division of the Superior Court that entered the deferred sentence**, under the existing docket — it is not a new case, and nothing is paid.
5. **After the order issues, the court sends copies** to each agency, department or official named in it — not you.

## Things the platform deliberately left blank

- **Your signature and its date.** Yours alone.
- **Every case fact.** The discharge order, the Restitution Unit statement and the record search hold them; the packet holds none of them.
- **Anything about good cause.** The request makes no argument about good cause and does not assert that none exists — if the court raises it, that conversation needs counsel.

## When to stop and get help instead

- the court has made, or signals it will make, a finding of good cause against expungement;
- the offence requires sex offender registration — the § 7041(h) confidential VCIC special index survives the expungement;
- you were not discharged, or the adjudication was not struck;
- restitution remains outstanding;
- any immigration question is involved.

## Vermont's words, and one federal caveat

After 1 July 2025 Vermont is a **sealing** state, and participant copy must not use expungement as the umbrella term — but this relief arises under chapter 221 and the statute's own word for it is expunge, so this packet uses the statutory word for this relief and no other. Expunged and sealed records may still appear in a **federal** criminal background check; Vermont relief does not reach federal records.

## What this packet is not

Verification guidance plus a prepared written request. There is no Vermont Judiciary form for the request — that is why it is composed — and this is not legal advice, nothing is sent for you, and nothing here decides whether the court will enter the order.
