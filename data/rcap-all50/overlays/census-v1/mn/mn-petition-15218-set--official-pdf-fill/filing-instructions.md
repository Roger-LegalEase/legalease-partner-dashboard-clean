# Filing your Minnesota expungement packet

## Where it goes

File with the district court administrator in the county where the case was decided. The
caption of every form in this packet names that county and that court file number.

## The filing fee

A district court filing fee applies unless a statutory fee waiver or a granted FEE102 waiver
applies. This packet does not state the amount of the fee, because the amount is set by the
court and the committed record for this route does not carry it. Ask the court administrator
what the fee is, and whether the statutory waiver reaches your petition, before you pay it.

FEE102 (Affidavit to Request Fee Waiver, Minn. Stat. § 563.01) is included so that you can ask
for the fee to be waived. Where the court asks for further detail, it uses FEE103. FEE103 is not
in this packet.

## Service

EXP104 is the proof of service. It lists the agencies that must be served and the agencies to
serve if they are related to your case. Six of them are marked (Required) on the form itself.

This packet leaves EXP104 entirely blank below its caption, and that is deliberate. EXP104 says,
under penalty of perjury, that you HAVE served the parties you check at the addresses you list.
Until you have actually put the envelopes in the mail, none of that is true. Complete EXP104
after you mail, not before, and then file it with the court.

The committed record for this route does not state a service method, a service deadline or a
filing deadline, so this packet states none. Ask the court administrator.

## The hearing

The court sets a hearing and fills in the notice at the top of EXP102 page 1. EXP102 also
carries a printed notice to law enforcement, government agencies and the prosecutor that any
objection must be filed as soon as possible and within 60 days.

If an agency or the prosecuting authority objects, the court sets a contested hearing.
Automated assistance ends there and you need a lawyer.

## What each document is

- **EXP102** Notice of Hearing and Petition for Expungement — primary filing.
- **EXP104** Proof of Service — certificate of service.
- **EXP106** Order Concerning Sealing/Expunging of Records (Minn. Stat. § 609A.02, subd. 1 or 2) — proposed order.
- **FEE102** Affidavit to Request Fee Waiver — fee waiver.
