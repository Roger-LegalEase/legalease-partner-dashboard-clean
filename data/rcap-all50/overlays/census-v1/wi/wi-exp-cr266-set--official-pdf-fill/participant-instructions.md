# Filing instructions — Wisconsin petition to expunge a conviction record (CR-266)

This packet is two Wisconsin circuit court forms, filed together in the criminal case you already have:

- **CR-266**, _Petition to Expunge Court Record of Conviction (Non-Probation/Non-Incarceration)_ — what you file.
- **CR-267**, _Order on Petition to Expunge Court Record of Conviction (Non-Probation/Non-Incarceration)_ — the proposed order you file with the petition, for the court to enter its decision on.

Both are prepared under Wis. Stat. § 973.015; Wisconsin circuit court forms CR-266 and CR-267.

The platform filled in only what it holds about you and your case — your name, your date of birth, your address, your e-mail, your telephone number, the county and the case number — on the caption of both forms, and your printed name and contact details in the declaration block of CR-266. Everything else on both forms is either already printed by the court or belongs to someone else, and this page says which is which.

## Why these two forms and not others

> CR-266 is only for adult conviction cases where expungement was already ordered and there was no probation/incarceration.

That is the whole of the choice. **CR-266 has no boxes to tick and no options to choose** — no fillable fields, no check boxes, nothing electable anywhere on the page. Which form you use IS the choice, and it was made from four facts about how your sentence ended:

| The fact | What your record has to say | Where it shows on CR-266 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expungement was ordered at sentencing | yes | paragraph 2 |
| You were placed on probation | no | paragraph 3 |
| You were sentenced to jail or prison | no | paragraph 4 |
| You completed the sentence, money included | yes | paragraph 5 |

If any one of those four is not what your record says, **this is the wrong form** and this packet is not built. That is checked before anything is drawn, and the build stops rather than guessing.

## The at-sentencing question, before anything else

> Wisconsin usually requires adult expungement to be ordered at sentencing. If it was not ordered then, there may not be a later petition route for that conviction, unless a special trafficking-victim route applies.

So the first thing to do with this packet is not to fill anything in. It is to get the **judgment of conviction**, and the **sentencing transcript or minutes** if the judgment is silent, and read whether the judge ordered expungement at sentencing. Paragraph 2 of CR-266 says that they did, and you sign it.

## What you are signing

CR-266 carries six numbered declarations. They are **already printed** — there is nothing to fill in and nothing to leave blank in them, and you adopt all six the moment you sign. The form says how seriously: _"I declare under the criminal penalty of false swearing that the information I have provided is true and accurate."_ Read each one against your own record before you sign:

1. I am the person convicted in this criminal case. I am asking that the court record of my criminal conviction be expunged. I understand that if the record is expunged:
   -  only the court record of the conviction will be expunged;
   -  other court records and records of the police, the Crime Information Bureau, other law enforcement agencies, and the district attorney will not be affected, AND
   -  the conviction is not vacated or set aside.
2. At the time the sentence was imposed, the court ordered that the record be expunged upon successful completion of the sentence.
3. I was not placed on probation.
4. I was not sentenced to jail or prison.
5. I have successfully completed my sentence in this case, including payment of all court-ordered financial obligations.
6. Between the date of conviction in this case and successful completion of my sentence, I was not convicted of another criminal offense.

Nothing was written on any of them, and neither the signature line nor the date beside it was filled in. Both are yours, and the date is the day you actually sign.

## What belongs to the court, on CR-267

CR-267 is the court's order and only its caption was filled in — the county, your name, your date of birth and the case number. Everything below the caption is the judge's, including **eight printed check boxes** this packet does not mark:

- **THE COURT FINDS** — beside _4. The court conducted a hearing on_
- **THE COURT ORDERS** — beside _1. GRANTED. The clerk is ordered to expunge the court's record of the conviction._
- **THE COURT ORDERS** — beside _2. DENIED because_
- **THE COURT ORDERS — 2. DENIED because** — beside _at the time the sentence was imposed, the court did not order that the record be expunged upon successful completion of the sentence._
- **THE COURT ORDERS — 2. DENIED because** — beside _the defendant did not successfully complete sentence because he or she failed to pay all court-ordered financial obligations._
- **THE COURT ORDERS — 2. DENIED because** — beside _the defendant did not successfully complete sentence because he or she failed to:_
- **THE COURT ORDERS — 2. DENIED because** — beside _between the date of conviction in this case and completion of the sentence, the defendant was convicted of another criminal offense._
- **THE COURT ORDERS — 2. DENIED because** — beside _Other:_

So are the three blank lines on it: the date of any hearing, the reason under _"failed to:"_, and the line after _"Other:"_. Leave all of them alone. The court also prints, at the foot of both forms: _"This form shall not be modified. It may be supplemented with additional material."_

## What you must do before you file

1. **Get the document** — Obtain Judgment of conviction, or the sentencing transcript or minutes. Ask the clerk of circuit court for the judgment of conviction, and the sentencing transcript or minutes where the judgment is silent. This is the document that proves the expungement was ordered at sentencing.
2. **Check your answer** — Check your answer to "Did the judge order at your sentencing that the record would be expunged once you completed your sentence?" against Judgment of conviction, or the sentencing transcript or minutes, and correct the packet if they disagree.
3. **Get the document** — Obtain Proof that the sentence was completed. Ask the clerk for records showing any fine, costs or other sentence terms were satisfied.
4. **Check your answer** — Check your answer to "On what date did you finish the sentence?" against Proof that the sentence was completed, and correct the packet if they disagree.
5. **Fill in** — Petitioner's signature and date — CR-266, signature block.
6. **Fill in** — Judge's signature and date of entry — CR-267, signature block.
7. **Fill in** — Any assertion that the sentencing court ordered expungement — CR-266, grounds.
8. **Fill in** — Any benefit-and-no-harm assertion — CR-266, grounds.
9. **Sign** — The petitioner signs CR-266.
10. **Filing fee** — Not confirmed. Because the filing is made in the existing criminal case rather than opening a new civil action, a filing fee may not apply. Verify with the clerk before quoting a price.
11. **Fee waiver** — none identified

Then, once all of that is done:

- **Serve** — none identified beyond ordinary circuit court practice.
- **File** — File CR-266 with the proposed CR-267 order in the existing circuit court criminal case in the county of conviction.

Two of those lines are the record saying it has nothing: the fee waiver reads _"none identified"_ and service reads _"none identified beyond ordinary circuit court practice"_. That is what the record establishes and this packet does not fill either gap in with a rule of its own.

### Three of those items name something that is not a blank on these forms

- _"Judge's signature and date of entry — CR-267, signature block."_ **CR-267 has no signature block.** The pinned form was searched for the words "judge", "by the court", "signature" and "dated" and prints none of them; below the denial reasons it prints only _"THIS IS A FINAL ORDER FOR THE PURPOSE OF APPEAL."_ and the distribution list. Nothing was left blank for a judge's signature because the form leaves no room for one.
- _"Any assertion that the sentencing court ordered expungement — CR-266, grounds."_ **That assertion is paragraph 2, and it is already printed.** CR-266 has no grounds section and no blank there. You make the assertion by signing the form, which is why the judgment of conviction or the sentencing transcript has to be in your hand before you do.
- _"Any benefit-and-no-harm assertion — CR-266, grounds."_ **CR-266 has no such assertion and no grounds section.** Under § 973.015 the finding that the person will benefit and society will not be harmed is the SENTENCING court's, made when expungement was ordered. There is nothing on this petition for you to assert about it and nothing was left blank for it.

## What this packet is not

This is a prepared set of official Wisconsin circuit court forms. It is not legal advice, it is not filed for you, and it does not decide whether your record will be expunged. CR-267 prints four named reasons a court may give for denying this petition, and an "Other:" line for a reason of its own. The decision is the court's.
