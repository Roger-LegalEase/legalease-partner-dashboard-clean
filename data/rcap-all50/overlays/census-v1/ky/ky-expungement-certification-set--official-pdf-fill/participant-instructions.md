# Kentucky expungement certification request - ky_expungement_certification-set

## Route selected

Request the statewide Kentucky expungement certification from the Administrative Office of the Courts Records Unit by mail or through the official AOC record-request portal.

## Get your conviction records first

Obtain Records of the convictions you want cleared. Ask the circuit court clerk in the county where the case was heard for the case number and the disposition. You need these to describe the records on the request form.

This applies where you cannot supply the case number or the exact charge from memory. Those facts go in the form's optional Additional Information box, which prints its own instruction: "(OPTIONAL) For Expungement Certification requests only, provide additional information to identify the type of case(s)/charge(s) to be expunged. If available, provide the county of origin, approximate date, type of case(s)/charge(s) and case number(s)."

## What it costs, and the exemption question

$40 for the certification.

AOC-RU-009 refers on its face to an exemption of fees if applicable. The criteria are not published and are recorded as an open question.

The form puts it in its own words: "I have provided the basic information necessary to qualify for record processing and exemption of fees - if applicable."

This packet does not tell you who qualifies for that exemption or how to ask, because the controlling record does not. The question it records, unanswered, is: "Does the AOC Records Unit waive or reduce the $40 expungement certification fee on indigency, and what does the participant have to supply to ask?" Until it is answered, treat the $40 as payable: the form prints "A fee of $40.00 is to be paid to the order of the KENTUCKY STATE TREASURER by check or money order ONLY. FAILURE TO COMPLY WITH THESE PROCEDURES WILL RESULT IN THE REQUEST BEING RETURNED UNPROCESSED."

## Send or request online

Mail the completed AOC-RU-009 with a $40 check or money order payable to the Kentucky State Treasurer to Administrative Office of the Courts, Records Unit, 1001 Vandalay Drive, Frankfort, Kentucky 40601. The official AOC record-request portal is an alternative channel for the same certification and requires an account and card payment.

The filing destination is the Administrative Office of the Courts, Records Unit. 1001 Vandalay Drive, Frankfort, Kentucky 40601. The participant may instead request the certification through the official AOC record request portal at kcoj.kycourts.net/RecordRequest, which requires an account and takes card payment; that is the same request through a different channel and produces the same certification. LegalEase completes the participant's portion of AOC-RU-009 and explains both channels.

## Timing

There is no waiting period to request the certification. After it is issued, the certification must reach the circuit court clerk with the later expungement filing within 30 days of receipt.

Processing takes roughly four to five months. The packet waits on the certification; the participant is told to expect that and is reminded when the 30-day filing window opens.

## Stop and get help

The controlling record declares these stop conditions. They are printed here in the record's own words.

- The certification comes back saying the participant is not eligible and the participant wants to file anyway. The clerk must still accept the petition, but whether to file against an adverse certification is a decision that needs a lawyer.
  An adverse certification is advisory. The Clerks' Manual directs the clerk to file the petition regardless; the judge decides eligibility.
- The certification lists convictions the participant says are not theirs or are wrong.
  The form gives the AOC's own channel for a record it believes is wrong: "If you suspect information contained on the record is incorrect, please contact the Records Unit at (502) 573-1682 or (800) 928-6381." Use it, and get help as well: the controlling record lists this as a point to stop and get help.
- The 30-day filing window has run before the participant could file.
