# Your Kentucky protective-order expungement motion

This is a motion asking a Kentucky court to expunge the record of a protective-order case that
was dismissed without a full order having issued, under KRS 403.745 and KRS 456.070. It is one
page. It is prepared for you to check, complete, sign and file. Nothing in it has been filed and
no court has decided anything.

## What is in the packet

- **AOC-275.18 (Rev. 1-16, Doc. Code EXPG)** — Motion for Expungement of Emergency/Temporary Order of Protection. 1 page.

your case papers. These are the record's own words, from

## What this motion does not reach

The committed legal-design record for this route carries these as instructions for this packet.
They are the record's own words.

- This clears the civil protective-order case record only. It does not touch any criminal record arising from the same events.
- No expungement certification is required for this motion.

## When this packet stops being the right tool

The same record lists the points at which this route stops being something you can do on your own.
If any of them describes your case, talk to a lawyer rather than filing this motion. These are the
record's own words, and the record writes about you in the third person as "the participant".

Its stop conditions:

- A full domestic violence order or non-temporary interpersonal protective order was issued in the case.
- Any order of protection issued against the participant in the preceding six months.
- A case that also involved criminal charges the participant wants cleared, which route to the criminal tracks.
- Any dispute about good cause.

And the boundary it draws on automated help:

- Any contested good-cause question ends automated assistance.
