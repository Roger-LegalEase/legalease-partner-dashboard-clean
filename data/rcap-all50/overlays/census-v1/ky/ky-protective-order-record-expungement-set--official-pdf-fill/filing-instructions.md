# Filing your Kentucky protective-order expungement motion

## Where it goes

File it in the protective-order case itself, with the Office of the Circuit Court Clerk for the
court that heard the petition. The caption of this motion names that case.

## What the clerk does

The committed record for this route says the clerk verifies your signature, applies the filed
stamp, completes the notification-of-hearing section on the face of the form, creates the
scheduled event and requests an updated criminal and protective-order history for the respondent.

## The filing fee

The committed record says the Clerks' Manual records no filing fee for this motion. It does not
record a fee-waiver treatment, and this packet states no fee amount. Ask the clerk.

## Service

The committed record says you may serve copies of the notice by first class mail per the
distribution list, and that otherwise the clerk does. The form's own footer lists the copies:
the court file, the petitioner and the respondent. The record does not state a service deadline
or a filing deadline, so this packet states none.

## Signing

You sign and date the motion on the printed lines beneath the three numbered statements, and
write your address and telephone number on the three rules below your signature. The record says
the clerk verifies the signature. No notarization is required on the face of the form.
