# Filing instructions — clear a West Virginia conviction on the accelerated § 61-11-26a route

This packet is built for **one** route: the accelerated expungement West Virginia allows where you have complied with an approved treatment or recovery programme, or graduated from an approved job readiness course, or both. It is not the ordinary waiting-period route.

The route record states the clock this way: _"ninety days of programme compliance OR completion of an approved job readiness adult training course, or both where applicable; the accelerated clock replaces the § 61-11-26(b)(1) one-year wait for a single misdemeanour"_

## Which of the two petitions you file — the packet does not choose

- **SCA-C906** — Used where the convictions to be expunged are misdemeanours or traffic citations, whether one or several.
- **SCA-C907** — Used instead where the conviction to be expunged is a nonviolent felony under § 61-11-26a(a)(3).

**File exactly one of them.** Whether a felony is a *nonviolent* felony is a legal characterisation of your own record, and this packet's own record names it a manual completion item, so nothing here decides it for you. The two forms are not interchangeable: they recite different waiting periods, and their certificates of service do not even list the same recipients.

## The documents you must have before you file

1. **Obtain Documentation of compliance with an approved substance abuse treatment or recovery and counselling programme. Ask the programme provider for written documentation of your compliance, covering the dates you were in the programme, and ask them to confirm in writing that the programme is approved by the Secretary of the Department of Health. Section 61-11-26a(b)(1) requires this to be included in the petition.**
   - Where to get it: The treatment or recovery programme provider, which must be approved by the Secretary of the Department of Health
   - When it applies: Only where the participant relies on treatment or recovery compliance under § 61-11-26a(a).
2. **Obtain Certificate of graduation from an approved job readiness adult training course. Ask the course provider for your certificate of graduation and for written confirmation that the course is approved by the West Virginia Department of Education. Section 61-11-26a(b)(2) requires the certificate to be included in the petition.**
   - Where to get it: The course provider, under a West Virginia Department of Education approved course
   - When it applies: Only where the participant relies on job readiness graduation under § 61-11-26a(a).
3. **Obtain Medical documentation of a history of substance abuse. Ask your treating clinician or the treatment provider for records documenting the history of substance abuse. LegalEase does not see, hold or assess these records; you attach them to the petition yourself.**
   - Where to get it: The treating clinician, treatment provider or medical records department
   - When it applies: Only on the treatment branch, where § 61-11-26a(a)(1) requires a medically documented history of substance abuse alongside compliance.
4. **Obtain Certified disposition, judgment order and sentencing order for every conviction. Ask the clerk of the county of conviction for certified copies for each case, and check the dates against the answers you gave us.**
   - Where to get it: Circuit or magistrate clerk of the county of conviction

The route record names the attachment requirement in terms: _"Section 61-11-26a(b)(2) requires the certificate to be included in the petition."_

## Signing, swearing and serving

- **Signing:** _"The petitioner signs the petition and the supplemental pleading, swears the verification before a notary public or other official, and signs and dates the certificate of service."_
- **Notarisation:** _"Required on the verification page of whichever official petition is used."_ The verification page carries **no fillable boxes at all** for the notarial block — the notary completes it on paper.
- **Service:** _"The petitioner serves the five § 61-11-26(e) recipients. The prosecuting attorney serves identified victims."_
- **The certificate of service is the last thing you complete.** It states that you have ALREADY given a copy to each recipient. Everything on that page is blank on your copy — including your own name — because when this packet was prepared you had not served anyone. Fill it in, tick the method and sign it on the day you actually serve.

## Where it goes and what it costs

- **Where:** _"The circuit court or circuit courts in which the conviction or convictions occurred, § 61-11-26a(a)."_
- **Fee:** _"The $100 State Police processing fee otherwise required by § 61-11-26(n) is waived on a § 61-11-26a petition."_
- **Fee:** _"The circuit clerk charges the § 59-1-11(a)(1) civil-action fee, which the official text sets at $200."_
- **Waiver:** _"The $100 State Police processing fee is waived by § 61-11-26a(c). The $200 circuit clerk civil filing fee is not waived by the section."_
- **Filing:** _"File the verified official petition — SCA-C906 for misdemeanours and traffic citations, SCA-C907 for a nonviolent felony — with the § 61-11-26a supplemental pleading, the verification notarised and the certificate of service completed, with the clerk of the circuit court of the county of conviction, once the applicable accelerated period has run."_
- **If it is opposed:** _"If a victim or the prosecuting attorney files a notice of opposition, or the court sets the matter for hearing, automated assistance ends and the participant needs a lawyer. The initial packet still generates."_
