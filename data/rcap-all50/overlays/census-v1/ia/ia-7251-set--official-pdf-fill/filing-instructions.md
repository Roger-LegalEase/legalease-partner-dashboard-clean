# Filing instructions

File this application in the Iowa District Court county and case shown on the criminal court record. No filing fee or proposed order is established by the held source.

If filing electronically, the e-filing system serves the county attorney. If filing on paper, first mail or deliver a copy to the county attorney and truthfully complete and sign the certificate of service on page 2. The county attorney has 20 days after service to respond. Keep a copy of the filed packet and certificate.
