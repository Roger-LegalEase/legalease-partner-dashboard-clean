# Iowa Rule 2.86 Form 5 application

## Eligibility and required review

Use this packet only for a conviction under Iowa Code section 725.1 when you were under age 18 at the time of the offense and two years have elapsed since the conviction, with no criminal conviction during the two-year period following that conviction other than a local traffic violation or a simple misdemeanor violation under Iowa Code chapter 321. Compare the conviction date and case number against the court record before filing. Stop for legal help if age, charge, trafficking, coercion, exploitation, or a later conviction is uncertain.

Form 5 item 2 is intentionally left blank for your personal review and completion. The age question concerns when the act occurred, not your age now or at conviction. An intake confirmation does not complete this court-facing declaration.

## Required before filing

- Item 2: age at the time of the act: personally review and complete item 2 only if you can truthfully confirm that you were under 18 at the time of the act; do not infer this from your current age or age at conviction, and seek legal assistance if uncertain without narrating the underlying facts.
- Representation choice: select the box that accurately states whether you are self-represented or represented by an attorney.
- Certificate service method: if filing on paper, state whether you mailed or delivered the application after completing service.
- Certificate recipient name and mailing address: if filing on paper, enter the name and mailing address of the county attorney actually served.

Review the completed application, then personally sign and date it. Do not include protected information in a public filing.
