# What you must do — checking that an old Rhode Island marijuana possession record has been cleared, and asking the court in writing where it has not, under R.I. Gen. Laws § 12-1.3-5

This packet is prepared for **Automatic Expungement of Marijuana Possession Records, R.I. Gen. Laws § 12-1.3-5**.

**Verify first, always.** Rhode Island completed more than 23,000 marijuana possession expungements in June 2023, over a year ahead of the statutory deadline, so an eligible record has almost certainly already been cleared. The first step is to pull your own BCI criminal history and look — never to file. The expedited written request in this packet is for the one situation where your own record shows the case still appears.

The conditions are **possession only** and **since decriminalized** — not every marijuana case. The section's definition of conviction also reaches a nolo plea with a jail or suspended jail sentence and an incomplete deferred sentence agreement with the Attorney General.

| Document | Where you get it |

## The items you must supply (request stage only)

Each is printed on the request as a labelled dotted blank.

### ri_marijuana-primary-filing-4 — Expedited Written Request for Expungement Under R.I. Gen. Laws Sec. 12-1.3-5

| The blank on the document | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| The tribunal this request is addressed to, written at the top - the one in which the marijuana case was heard | which tribunal heard the marijuana case - Superior Court, District Court, or the Traffic Tribunal - written at the top of the request; your BCI record or your old papers show it |
| Case number or citation number, as it appears on your records, if one appears | the case number or citation number as it appears on your BCI record or court papers, if one appears - if none does, write none |
| Year of the case, approximately | roughly what year the case was |
| How the case ended - a civil violation, a conviction, a plea of nolo contendere with a jail or suspended jail sentence, or a deferred sentence agreement whose period of deferment has not been completed | how the case ended, in the section's own categories - note that the section's definition of conviction includes a nolo plea with a jail or suspended jail sentence and an incomplete deferred sentence agreement |
| Amount of marijuana stated in the record, if the record states one | the amount the record states, if it states one - where it does not, the section presumes two ounces or less, and you write nothing |

## What you do, in order (request stage)

1. **Confirm from your own BCI record** that the marijuana case still appears. This packet does not certify that the automatic relief failed — the confirmation is yours, from your record.
2. **Fill in every dotted blank** from your BCI record and old papers. Do not guess.
3. **Sign and date the request yourself** — and only after actually checking, because your signature makes its statements your own.
4. **Send the request to the court in which the marijuana case was heard**, and keep a copy of everything. **Nothing is paid**: eligible marijuana-related fines, fees and costs are waived, and all court costs are waived for anyone who was incarcerated for marijuana possession.
5. **After the court acts, the court delivers the order** to the Attorney General, the arresting agency and any other agency known to hold records — not you.
6. **If the court denies the request or does not answer**: stop and escalate to a lawyer or legal-aid office. The statute identifies no remedy and no appeal route for the expedited procedure, so do not re-file.

## Things the platform deliberately left blank

- **Your signature and its date.** Yours alone, and only after the BCI check.
- **Every case fact.** Your BCI record and old papers hold them; the packet holds none of them.

## When to stop and get help instead

- the case had non-marijuana counts you also want cleared;
- the charge was possession with intent to distribute, or distribution;
- any dispute about possession-only status or the amount;
- the record is federal, out-of-state, military or tribal;
- the court denies or does not answer the expedited request.

## What this packet is not

Verification guidance plus a prepared written request. There is no official Rhode Island form for the request — that is why it is composed — and this is not legal advice, nothing is sent for you, and nothing here decides eligibility. Where the record has already cleared, nothing in this packet is for sending at all.
