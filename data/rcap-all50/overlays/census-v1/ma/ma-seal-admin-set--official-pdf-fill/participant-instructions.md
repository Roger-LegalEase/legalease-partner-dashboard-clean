# Filing instructions — seal a Massachusetts adult conviction or juvenile record

This packet is the Massachusetts Office of the Commissioner of Probation's **PETITION TO SEAL** (form footer `OCP 4/12`). It is one page, and it goes to one office — no court, no docket, no caption.

## The one decision only you can make

Part A of the petition offers four boxes, and **you must tick the one your own record fits.** This packet has deliberately ticked **none** of them, and you should know why: this petition covers **two** different routes — sealing an **adult conviction** under G.L. c. 276 § 100A, and sealing a **juvenile record** under § 100B — and which one you are on, and whether your offence counts as a misdemeanor or a felony, are facts about your record that the platform has never seen.

The repository's own route record treats that question the same way. It lists the **felony or misdemeanour characterisation** as something completed later: _"Felony or misdemeanour characterisation where it cannot be ascertained"_

**A packet that guessed here would be guessing on a document you sign under the penalties of perjury.** So the box is yours. Use the table below to find yours.

### Which box, and how long you must have waited

| Tick this box | It covers | What to do next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Box 1** | Part A box 1 — Section 100B, Chapter 276: delinquency (juvenile) cases completed 3 years before this request (selection) | tick box 1 if the record you are asking to seal is a JUVENILE delinquency case and every sentence element of it, and of any later court appearance, was completed at least 3 years ago. If you tick it, sign numbered Affidavit 1 below. |
| **Box 2** | Part A box 2 — Section 100A, Chapter 276: misdemeanor cases completed 3 years before this request (selection) | tick box 2 if the record is an ADULT MISDEMEANOR — or was a felony when committed and is a misdemeanor now — and every sentence element of it, and of any later court appearance, was completed at least 3 years ago. If you tick it, sign numbered Affidavit 2 below. |
| **Box 3** | Part A box 3 — Section 100A, Chapter 276: felony cases completed 7 years before this request, 15 for eligible sex offenses (selection) | tick box 3 if the record is an ADULT FELONY and every sentence element of it, and of any later court appearance, was completed at least 7 years ago — 15 years for an eligible sex offense. If you tick it, sign numbered Affidavit 3 below. |

The waiting periods the route record sets out — read these against your own record before you tick anything:

| The kind of record | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Felony | 7 years |
| Misdemeanour, from the court appearance and disposition including any incarceration or custody | 3 years |
| Offence that cannot be ascertained to have been a felony when committed | Treated as a misdemeanour, so 3 years |
| Sex offence under c. 6 § 178C | 15 years, or for as long as the person is under a duty to register in Massachusetts or any other state where they reside or would be if residing here, whichever is longer |
| Violation of G.L. c. 209A § 7 or c. 258E § 9 | Treated as a felony, so 7 years |

### Then sign the matching affidavit

The petition's printed instruction reads **"SELECT appropriate box(es). If 1, 2, or 3 are selected you must sign the corresponding numbered Affidavit below."** Whichever Part A box you tick, tick and sign the **numbered affidavit with the same number** underneath. Each affidavit is a sworn statement about your own record, which is why this packet leaves every one of them to you:

| The affidavit | It answers |
| --- | --- |
| Affidavit 1 | Part A box 1, the juvenile delinquency branch |
| Affidavit 2 | Part A box 2, the adult misdemeanor branch |
| Affidavit 3 | Part A box 3, the adult felony branch |

**Do not tick more than one Part A box unless more than one is genuinely true of your record**, and sign every affidavit you tick.

## Where it goes

The petition is addressed on its own face: **Commissioner of Probation, One Ashburton Place, Room 405, Boston, MA 02108**.

The route record names the same office: _"Not applicable. One filing to the Office of the Commissioner of Probation covers the statewide record. No court and no caption."_ It records the court and caption question as not applicable, because on this route there is no court and no caption — one filing to one office covers the whole Massachusetts record.

## Service, fees and hearings

- **Who you must serve: nobody.** The route record answers both the recipient and the method question with the same two words — _"No service."_. You send the petition to the Commissioner's office and to no one else.
- **Fee waiver:** _"Not applicable."_
- **Filing fee:** the route record does not record one and this form prints none, so **no amount is stated here**. Ask the Office of the Commissioner of Probation — the same office the petition goes to — whether any fee applies. An unsourced figure in a filing instruction is worse than none.
- **Hearing:** the route record does not record a hearing step for these routes, and none is stated here.

## What you must do, in order

1. **Tick the one Part A box your record fits** — box 1, 2 or 3, using the tables above.
2. **Fill in every item in the table below**, from your own papers.
3. **Tick and sign the numbered affidavit with the same number as your Part A box.**
4. **Sign the petition** on the `Petitioner's Signature` line. The route record: _"Required, under the penalties of perjury."_
5. **Write nothing below the line that reads `PETITIONER NOT TO WRITE BELOW THIS LINE`.** That block is the Commissioner's.
6. **Send it to Commissioner of Probation, One Ashburton Place, Room 405, Boston, MA 02108.**

## Blanks the form itself is too small for

On some records a detail the platform holds is longer than the box the form prints for it. The packet leaves that box **blank rather than shortening what you told us** — a shortened address on a sworn petition reads as a complete one. If any of these is blank on your copy, write it in by hand:

## What the platform did not fill in, and why

- **Your name.** The form gives `Last Name`, `First Name` and `Middle Name` three separate captions but only **one** box to write them in. A single box takes one value written from its left edge, so anything the platform wrote would sit under `Last Name` whatever it contained. Print your name yourself, in the three captions' order.
- **Alias/maiden/previous name, your father's name, your mother's maiden name, your husband's or wife's name.** The platform does not collect any of them. It also refused to let its own field matcher fill them: that matcher resolves all four of those boxes to **your own legal name**, which on a petition you sign would be false.
- **Your Social Security number.** The platform does not hold it and does not print government identifiers on any form.
- **Occupation and place of birth.** The platform does not collect either.
- **Your signature and the affidavit signatures.** You sign; the platform never signs for you.

## What this packet is not

This is a prepared copy of an official Massachusetts form. It is not legal advice, it is not filed for you, and it does not decide whether your record can be sealed — the Commissioner of Probation decides that.
