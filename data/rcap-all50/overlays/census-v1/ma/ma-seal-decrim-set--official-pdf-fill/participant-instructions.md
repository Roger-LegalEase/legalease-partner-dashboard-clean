# Filing instructions — seal a Massachusetts record of an offense that is no longer a crime

This packet is the Massachusetts Office of the Commissioner of Probation's **PETITION TO SEAL** (form footer `OCP 4/12`). It is one page, and it goes to one office — no court, no docket, no caption.

## Which box this packet ticks, and why

Part A of the petition offers four boxes and you must select the one your record fits. **This packet has already ticked box 4** — _Section 100A, Chapter 276: a recorded offense which is no longer a crime, except where the elements of the offense continue to be a crime under a different designation._

That is the route this packet was built for. The repository's route record for this route states it in terms: _"agency — Office of the Commissioner of Probation — Same agency filing as the ordinary § 100A route, using Part A box 4."_

**Boxes 1, 2 and 3 are deliberately left unticked.** They are the waiting-period branches — juvenile cases under § 100B at three years, misdemeanors under § 100A at three years, felonies under § 100A at seven — and they belong to a different route. This one has no waiting period at all. The route record's own words: _"A recorded offence that is no longer a crime — None. This is the only administrative sealing route with no waiting period."_

The petition's printed instruction reads **"SELECT appropriate box(es). If 1, 2, or 3 are selected you must sign the corresponding numbered Affidavit below."** Box 4 is not one of those three, so **none of the numbered affidavits below applies to you** and all three are left unticked and unsigned.

**Read box 4 before you file it.** It covers a recorded offense that is no longer a crime — _except_ where the elements of that offense are still a crime under a different name. Only you can say whether that describes your record; if it does not, this is the wrong petition and you should not file it.

## Where it goes

The petition is addressed on its own face: **Commissioner of Probation, One Ashburton Place, Room 405, Boston, MA 02108**.

The route record names the same office: _"Not applicable. Office of the Commissioner of Probation."_ It records the court and caption question as not applicable, because on this route there is no court and no caption — one filing to one office covers the whole Massachusetts record.

## Service, fees and hearings

- **Who you must serve: nobody.** The route record answers both the recipient and the method question with the same two words — _"No service."_. You send the petition to the Commissioner's office and to no one else.
- **Fee waiver:** _"Not applicable."_
- **Filing fee:** the route record does not record one and this form prints none, so **no amount is stated here**. Ask the Office of the Commissioner of Probation — the same office the petition goes to — whether any fee applies. An unsourced figure in a filing instruction is worse than none.
- **Hearing:** the route record does not record a hearing step for this route, and none is stated here.

## What you must do, in order

1. **Check box 4 is right for your record** — see the warning above.
2. **Fill in every item in the table below**, from your own papers.
3. **Sign the petition** on the `Petitioner's Signature` line. The route record: _"Required, under the penalties of perjury."_
4. **Leave the three numbered affidavits blank and unsigned** — they answer boxes 1, 2 and 3, which this petition does not select.
5. **Write nothing below the line that reads `PETITIONER NOT TO WRITE BELOW THIS LINE`.** That block is the Commissioner's.
6. **Send it to Commissioner of Probation, One Ashburton Place, Room 405, Boston, MA 02108.**

## What the platform did not fill in, and why

- **Your name.** The form gives `Last Name`, `First Name` and `Middle Name` three separate captions but only **one** box to write them in. A single box takes one value written from its left edge, so anything the platform wrote would sit under `Last Name` whatever it contained. Print your name yourself, in the three captions' order.
- **Alias/maiden/previous name, your father's name, your mother's maiden name, your husband's or wife's name.** The platform does not collect any of them. It also refused to let its own field matcher fill them: that matcher resolves all four of those boxes to **your own legal name**, which on a petition you sign would be false.
- **Your Social Security number.** The platform does not hold it and does not print government identifiers on any form.
- **Occupation and place of birth.** The platform does not collect either.
- **Your signature and the affidavit signatures.** You sign; the platform never signs for you.

## What this packet is not

This is a prepared copy of an official Massachusetts form. It is not legal advice, it is not filed for you, and it does not decide whether your record can be sealed — the Commissioner of Probation decides that.
