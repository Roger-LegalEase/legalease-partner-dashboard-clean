# Before you act - making the written administrative request to remove a qualifying DNA sample and DNA records from New Mexico's system and CODIS

This packet is prepared for **New Mexico DNA sample and profile expungement under NMSA 1978, Section 29-16-10**.

The PDF is a preparation guide composed from the committed legal records listed in the source receipt. It is not an official agency form, is not filed with a court, and does not replace any current form, portal, address, fee, or submission rule the receiving agency requires.

The platform wrote only the identity and contact facts it holds: full legal name, date of birth, mailing address, telephone, and email. It did not sign, date, notarize, or make a legal election for you.

## Required before you use the agency process

| Blank printed in the guide | What you must supply |
| --- | --- |
| Arresting agency and arrest date tied to the DNA collection | the agency and date from the arrest and DNA records; confirm it with the arresting agency and the participant's criminal-history record |
| Case court, county, case number, and disposition date | all identifiers exactly as the certified disposition states them; confirm it with the clerk of the court that disposed of the case |
| Prior-felony and pending-authorized-felony screen | confirmation that no disqualifying prior felony conviction or pending felony charge for which DNA collection is authorized exists; confirm it with the participant's complete criminal-history records and New Mexico counsel |
| Qualifying Section 29-16-10 branch | the exact qualifying event - reversal, dismissal, nolle prosequi, successful diversion, conditional discharge, misdemeanor conviction, acquittal, or the no-felony-charge-within-one-year branch; confirm it with the certified court disposition and a New Mexico attorney if classification is uncertain |
| Confirmed administrative center, destination, and accepted request format | the exact office, address or portal, required format, attachments, and fee if any; confirm it with the New Mexico Department of Public Safety |
| Certified disposition or sworn no-felony-charge affirmation | the certified disposition for the selected branch, or the sworn affirmation if the receiving office confirms that branch and format; confirm it with the court clerk and the confirmed administrative center |

## Route boundary

Do not use a Rule 1-077.1 court form and do not file this guide with a court. Confirm the state DNA identification system's administrative center and its accepted written-request format with DPS first.
Do not apply if a prior felony conviction or a pending felony charge authorizing DNA collection may exist; obtain counsel. Obtain certified documentation, or use a sworn affirmation only in the branch and form the confirmed receiving office accepts.
The recorded 30- or 45-day agency periods are post-request processing periods, never prefiling waits.

## Protected items left blank

Your signature, signature date, any notarization, and every agency-, clerk-, prosecutor-, court-, or hearing-officer field remain blank. Complete them only on the receiving authority's own instrument and only when that authority directs.
