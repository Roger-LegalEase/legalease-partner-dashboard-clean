# What you must do before you file — asking a Nebraska district court to expunge the record of an arrest made due to the error of a law enforcement agency, under Neb. Rev. Stat. § 29-3523(9)

This packet is prepared for **Petition to Expunge Criminal History Record Information After an Arrest Made in Error, Neb. Rev. Stat. § 29-3523(9)**.

There is no official Nebraska form for this petition — the memo this packet is built from records that an exhaustive search of the Master Forms List returns no form whose title contains Expunge — so the petition is a composed pleading grounded on the statute's recorded requirements and the recorded controlling decisions for this route.

This is the **only Nebraska adult route that expunges** a record. Every other Nebraska route uses different relief with different words; do not mix them up.

The platform filled in what it holds about you: your name, your mailing address, your telephone number and your email. Every case fact is a labelled dotted blank listed below, and you fill it from the record itself, never from memory.

## Where this is filed, and against whom

- **Venue**: the district court of the county in which the arrest occurred (the recorded controlling decision for this route). The petition opens a **new civil case** there.
- **Respondent**: the county attorney of that county (the recorded controlling decision). The service page explains how the respondent is served.

## Documents you must obtain

| Document | Where you get it |
| --- | --- |
| Nebraska State Patrol criminal history report, showing the arrest still appears — check your answers against it and correct the packet if they disagree | Nebraska State Patrol, limited criminal history search through nebraska.gov |
| Anything the agency sent you about the mistake — a correction notice, agency correspondence, identification records — if you hold any | the arresting agency, or your own papers; the platform names these and does not collect, inspect or judge them |

## The items you must supply

Each is printed on the petition as a labelled dotted blank. Fill every one.

## What you do, in order

1. **Have an attorney review the petition and your evidence.** The burden is clear and convincing evidence and the respondent is a public office. This is a strong recommendation, not a gate.
2. **Gather the records** listed above.
3. **Fill in every dotted blank.** Do not guess a date, an agency name or a case number.
4. **Ask the clerk of the district court** of the county of arrest what filing fee applies — no source this packet is built from establishes it — and how a new civil action is served on the county attorney there. A fee waiver is available in principle under Neb. Rev. Stat. § 25-2301.01 if you cannot pay.
5. **Sign and date the petition yourself.**
6. **File with the clerk of the district court** of the county of arrest, as a new civil matter.
7. **Serve the county attorney formally**, in the manner the clerk directs. A courtesy copy alone is not formal service.

## Things the platform deliberately left blank

- **Your signature, and the date beside it.** A signature is yours alone, and a date written before you sign would be false.
- **The case number of the petition itself.** The clerk assigns it at filing.
- **Every line of your account of the agency's error.** The petition prints your own first-hand account and asserts no legal conclusion about it.
- **The filing fee and the exact service mechanics.** Neither is established by the sources this packet is built from; the clerk of the district court of the county of arrest is the authority that can answer both.

## When to stop and get help instead of filing

- you cannot say, concretely, what the agency got wrong;
- you cannot identify the arresting agency;
- the arrest was lawful and simply did not lead to charges — that belongs to Nebraska's automatic non-conviction treatment, not this petition;
- the real cause was someone else's use of your identity;
- the county attorney appears and contests, or the court sets an evidentiary hearing.

## What this packet is not

This is a prepared composed pleading with process pages. It is not an official form — none exists for this petition — and it is not legal advice, it is not filed for you, and it does not decide whether the court will order the record expunged.
