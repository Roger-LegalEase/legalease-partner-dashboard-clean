## File one motion, not two

- Dismissed on the motion of the prosecuting attorney - ticked at CC 6:12, paragraph 2, "Were dismissed;"; CC 6:15.1, "on the motion of the prosecuting attorney;"
- Dismissed after completing a problem-solving-court programme - ticked at CC 6:12, paragraph 2, "Were dismissed;"; CC 6:15.1, "after completion of a program prescribed by a"; CC 6:15.1, "problem solving court."
- Acquitted - ticked at CC 6:12, paragraph 2, "Resulted in an acquittal;"; CC 6:15.1, "after acquittal;"

CC 6:12 is the primary motion. CC 6:15.1 is the conditional alternate, filed
only where the clerk prefers the statute-specific form. Each motion covers one
case; another case needs its own motion.
