# Filing instructions - il-exp-nonconv-set

File the Request, Case List, any Additional Cases forms and the proposed Order with the circuit clerk in each county where an arrest occurred or a charge was brought. E-file where the county requires it. AOIC instructs users to download the form in Adobe Acrobat or Reader before filling it and to flatten the PDF before e-filing, so any LegalEase output must be delivered pre-flattened.

The destination is the Circuit clerk of the county of arrest or charge. File the Request, Case List, any Additional Cases forms and the proposed Order with the circuit clerk in each county where an arrest occurred or a charge was brought. The clerk performs service under § 5.2(d)(4).

**Fees.** A circuit clerk fee plus an ISP processing fee, both county-collected. ISP charges nothing to file a petition and $60 to process a court order. County totals commonly run from roughly $60 to $235; McLean County publishes $136, comprising a $60 filing fee, a $60 state police fee and $16 for four certified copies. Treat every figure as county-specific.

**Waiver.** Supreme Court Rule 298 Application for Waiver of Court Fees. Section 5.2(d)(1) requires no fee where a Rule 298 order has been obtained or the fee is otherwise waived. Section 5.2(d)(10) waives the expungement fee entirely where the same record was previously sealed under this Section.

**Service.** The circuit court clerk serves, under § 5.2(d)(4). The participant does not serve anyone. Participant-facing instructions must never tell the participant to serve the State's Attorney, ISP, the arresting agency or the chief legal officer.

The judge or clerk completes the proposed order, the clerk-assigned case numbers, and the later-completion fields.
