# Filing instructions - il-seal-edu-set

File the Request, Case List, any Additional Cases forms and the proposed Order with the circuit clerk in each county where an arrest occurred or a charge was brought. E-file where the county requires it. AOIC requires the PDF to be flattened before e-filing, so LegalEase output is delivered pre-flattened.

The destination is the Circuit clerk of the county of arrest or charge. File with the circuit clerk in each county where an arrest occurred or a charge was brought. The clerk performs service under § 5.2(d)(4).

**Forms.** The packet carries the required EXP-AD Request, EXP-AD Case List, EXP-AD Order Granting, and EXP-AD Order Denying. When you request a fee waiver, it also carries the FW-CIV-APPLICATION and the official three-page FW-CIV-ORDER; include those two forms together. If you do not request a fee waiver, omit both.

**Fees.** A circuit clerk fee plus an ISP processing fee, both county-collected. ISP charges nothing to file a petition and $60 to process a court order. County totals commonly run from roughly $60 to $235; McLean County publishes $136. Treat every figure as county-specific.

**Waiver.** Supreme Court Rule 298 Application for Waiver of Court Fees. Section 5.2(d)(1) requires no fee where a Rule 298 order has been obtained or the fee is otherwise waived.

**Service.** The circuit court clerk serves, under § 5.2(d)(4). The participant serves no one.

The judge or clerk completes the proposed orders, the clerk-assigned case numbers, and the later-completion fields.
