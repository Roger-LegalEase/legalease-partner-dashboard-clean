# Filing instructions - il-cannabis-vacate-set

File the Motion, any Additional Cannabis Convictions form, the Notice of Court Date and the proposed Order with the circuit court, the Chief Judge, or a judge designated by the Chief Judge. Deliver the forms pre-flattened.

The destination is the Circuit court of the conviction, or the Chief Judge or a designated judge of that circuit. The circuit court clerk promptly serves the motion and supporting documentation on the State's Attorney, who may object within 60 days with supporting evidence.

**Fees.** The source review does not state a separate cannabis motion fee. Treat county filing charges as county-specific and confirm with the clerk. ISP charges $60 to process a court order.

**Waiver.** Supreme Court Rule 298 Application for Waiver of Court Fees where a county fee applies.

**Service.** Clerk service applies to the motion itself under § 5.2(i)(3). The cannabis suite additionally uses petitioner-completed Notice of Court Date forms, so the adult suite's clerk-service model does not carry over.

Do not complete the judge's order, clerk certification, hearing details, service details, signature, or signature date in advance.
