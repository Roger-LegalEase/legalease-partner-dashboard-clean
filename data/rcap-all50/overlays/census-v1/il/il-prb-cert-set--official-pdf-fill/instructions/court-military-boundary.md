# Court-stage diagnostic preview: certificate required

DIAGNOSTIC NON-FILING PREVIEW. This preview demonstrates the court forms for a hypothetical issued-certificate scenario. No actual Prisoner Review Board certificate is supplied or generated. Do not file these preview forms. The production stage refuses generation unless the participant supplies the actual issued certificate and its identity, case, branch and file hash are validated.

The preview illustrates the military expungement certificate under section 5.2(e-6) branch. The Request and Case List retain all official pages. The CE outcome and certificate option illustrate that branch only; they do not prove that the Board issued a certificate to this synthetic participant.

Before an actual court filing, obtain the issued certificate from the Board, verify the participant, conviction case and type of relief, and attach it. A Board application, acknowledgement, filing receipt or promise of a decision is not a certificate. Sealing and military expungement certificates are not interchangeable. A generic nonmilitary expungement-certificate application remains unavailable.

Use the circuit court of conviction: the Chief Judge, a designated judge, or in counties under 3,000,000 inhabitants the presiding trial judge. Confirm the court, county, filing address and method with the circuit clerk. The form-header Case Number is a new number if the clerk assigns one; the existing conviction number belongs in the case table and Case List.

The separate Case List must be filed at the same time as the Request. Every case listed in the Request must also appear there. Additional Arrests or Cases forms apply when a Request table needs more than ten rows; an additional Case List applies when more than seventy case numbers are needed. These one-case previews trigger none of those extra forms. Do not drop a charge or case to avoid a required continuation.

# Check the actual record and complete the filing

Obtain the ISP statewide criminal-history transcript through Access and Review, certified dispositions from the circuit clerk, and a Chicago RAP sheet if Chicago arrests occurred. Match names, aliases, birth date, all charges, arrest dates, arresting agencies and outcomes to those records and the issued certificate. Supporting external records are not created by this packet.

You and your legal adviser must confirm the relief requested for each case from the issued certificate. The exact June 2026 Case List contains case-number columns, not a per-case expunge/seal election column. Make the actual selections on the Request. Do not add a nonexistent column or treat this PRB branch as ordinary Illinois court expungement.

Personally review and sign the Request verification after all answers and the certificate attachment are correct. The source permits typing a signature when completing it online, but the preparation system leaves that act to you. Printed name and contact details are supplied. All attorney-only blocks and official receipt/signature areas stay blank for their proper actors.

No hearing date is supplied. The court sets a hearing if an objection is filed, with the adopted route requiring at least thirty days notice. Seek legal help for any objection or contested hearing. The Office of the State Appellate Defender, Expungement Unit, 866-787-1776, is the statewide referral destination.

# Fees, service and limits of relief

Ask the circuit clerk for the current county filing fee, total processing charges, copy count, accepted filing method and self-represented e-filing exemption process. There is no statewide county total established by the held materials. The held ISP fee schedule distinguishes no charge to file a petition from $60 to process a court order; the county collects the applicable charges. Do not confuse those events or quote a different county's amount as your fee.

If unable to pay, use the Supreme Court Rule 298 Application for Waiver of Court Fees and obtain the waiver order. Section 5.2(d)(1) requires no fee where the Rule 298 order has been obtained or the fee is otherwise waived. The preview does not manufacture a waiver application or order.

The circuit clerk performs court-stage service under section 5.2(d)(4); the participant serves no one in that stage. Earlier Board-stage copies to the sentencing/chief judge and State's Attorney are a different process. Ask the clerk how to retain filed copies, receive decisions and obtain the entered court order.

Understand the result before paying for help: a certificate alone does not clear a record or guarantee a court order. Sealing limits public access rather than destroying the record; authorized law enforcement and others permitted by law may retain access. Expungement differs from sealing and does not promise destruction of every copy everywhere. The actual order governs which records and relief are covered. No commercial or live route is enabled by these diagnostic previews.
