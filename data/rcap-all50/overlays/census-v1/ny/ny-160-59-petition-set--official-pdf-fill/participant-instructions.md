## Facts and documents for your application

Use the actual case number, court, conviction and sentence records for each case. Reuse known answers and correct disagreements; do not substitute arrest, conviction, sentencing and release dates for one another. A second-case or future-application row applies only when that case exists and belongs in that row. Several case numbers do not by themselves establish one criminal transaction. Prior CPL 160.58 orders, incarceration timing and transaction grouping remain subject to their separate record-specific review.

The application requires your own reasons for sealing. Do not leave the statement empty or invent rehabilitation facts. Supporting letters and other optional evidence can help but do not make every attachment line mandatory. Reuse an existing certificate of disposition for each case; request one only if needed, or provide the written explanation required by the form if you cannot obtain it. No signature, service event, notarization, certified-copy acquisition or upload becomes an intake or purchase gate merely because a later filing step requires it.

Answer prior and intended future applications separately. If you previously applied under CPL 160.58 or 160.59, attach the prior application, including one that was denied or remains pending; prior filing is not by itself a blanket denial. Give future-case details only when you actually intend another application.

## Signing and service depend on the actual form revision

The August 2026 packet uses signed, dated affirmations by the applicant and actual server. Additional statement pages must carry the required affirmation and signature. The retained older forms use notarized affidavits; do not remove their jurats or claim that the new form has already replaced these PDFs. Follow the current approved form's execution requirements when it is supplied. Neither execution method permits a fabricated signature or date.

Serve a complete copy before filing on each applicable prosecutor. This is usually the District Attorney; use the Attorney General or Special Narcotics Prosecutor when that office prosecuted the case. The applicant or another actual server may mail or personally deliver the papers under the source instructions. The current service affirmation states that the server is "over 18 years of age"; use an actual server who can truthfully make that declaration. Stamped in-person proof can satisfy the stated proof requirement; otherwise the actual server completes proof of service after serving. The current packet directs a separate service affirmation for each prosecutor served. Keep recipients, methods and dates distinct. Court, notary and prosecutor acts remain with their proper actors.

The retained six-page application's service instructions permit combined proof unless service occurred on different dates or by different people. The retained pro-se packet and current packet instead direct separate proof for each prosecutor. This source discrepancy remains under review; do not silently combine or substitute proofs across revisions.

The source application states a 45-day prosecutor response period. If the case is eligible and opposed, the court schedules a hearing; opposition alone does not establish eligibility. File in one appropriate court, using the most serious conviction or the most recent conviction of equal level as the source instructions direct. Keep copies and actual proof of filing.

## Costs and record requests

The retained compiled profile records no separate CPL 160.59 motion filing fee. That profile entry is not a new current fee approval. The July 2024 certificate-of-disposition request states $5 outside New York City and $10 in the five boroughs; ask the court about accepted payment methods and applicable fee-exempt or poor-person-order treatment. Do not mark a court-use fee, identity or authorization box yourself. A record-request charge is separate from a motion filing fee. Do not add a universal notary charge to the current affirmation packet.

On a certificate request, identify the actual requester. The defendant provides photo ID; a defendant's agent provides the defendant's written authorization. The sealed-case option applies to a defendant or agent who requests it. Name an agency only if actually represented, and choose the actual mail or pickup delivery preference. Do not borrow the defendant's contact details for another requester or mark the court's receipt/verification boxes.

## Where self-help ends

**This packet is not legal advice, and no lawyer has reviewed your case in preparing it.** It is a prepared set of official New York forms for you to read, complete, sign, complete the applicable affirmation or affidavit, serve and file yourself. It is not filed for you, and it does not decide whether your conviction can be sealed - that decision is the court's, and it is discretionary.

Stop and get a lawyer's help before you file if any of these is true of your case. Each one is recorded in this route's own track record as a point where self-help ends:

- any conviction that might fall on the exclusion list, including any attempt or conspiracy whose target offence has to be analysed;

- any class A felony, which is excluded here even where it would qualify under Clean Slate - the two lists are opposites and this is where the routing error happens;

- any argument that several crimes arose from a single criminal transaction and should count as one;

- District Attorney objection, which may require a hearing when the case is eligible;

- any pending or open criminal charge;

- more than two convictions, or more than one felony;

- the rehabilitation and interests-of-justice showing, which is the heart of a discretionary application and is not a form-filling exercise;

- immigration exposure. Sealing does not remove immigration consequences and sealed records remain reachable by immigration authorities. Ask an immigration attorney before you sign anything;

- firearms licensing goals, because sealed records remain available for firearms licensing;

- federal and out-of-state convictions, which New York sealing does not reach at all.

**Who to ask, for what.** The clerk's office of the court where you were convicted and sentenced answers procedural questions - what to file, where, and what the court needs. Only a lawyer admitted in New York can advise you on eligibility, on what to argue, or at a hearing. The compiled New York profile records that legal-aid organisations and county district attorney sealing units assist pro se applicants at no cost, and some county district attorney offices run sealing units that publish their own instructions.

The post-order seal-verification request is used only after an actual sealing order. It is not proof that filing an application sealed the record. Existing legal, source, bilingual and delivery approvals remain pending.
