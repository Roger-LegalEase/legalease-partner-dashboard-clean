# Hawaii Attorney General Expungement Application under § 831-3.2

**Route:** Non-conviction arrest-information expungement under HRS § 831-3.2
**Mechanism:** Nonconviction arrest expungement through the Attorney General (Haw. Rev. Stat. § 831-3.2)
**Form:** HCJDC-159B — HCJDC 159(b) Rev. 06/03/2026

## What this route is, in the record's own words

- A written HCJDC application may cover qualifying arrest information for which no conviction was secured.
- The source says the arrest record is removed from the arresting agency and statewide adult criminal-history repository, while court records may remain available.
- The source excludes felony or misdemeanor non-convictions caused by bail forfeiture, fugitives who made prosecution impossible, and Chapter 704 mental-disease dispositions.
- A complete application, valid government photo ID, payment, signature, mailing address, and self-addressed stamped envelope are required.

> Since July 1, 2025 the Attorney General process also addresses related court-record removal under the one-step procedure. The one-year deferred-acceptance and five-year bail-forfeiture exceptions must be coded separately and never applied to the general branch.

## Timing

- generally available on a qualifying nonconviction disposition, with two coded statutory exceptions: one year after discharge or dismissal in the specified deferred-acceptance branch, and five years in the specified petty-misdemeanor or violation bail-forfeiture branch
- This rule governs filings on or after 2025-07-01.
- Attorney General processing period — agency processing after the application, not a filing wait.

## When this route does not apply

The form itself prints the circumstances in which an expungement order **shall not** be issued. Read them on the application before you file. The record additionally names these as the points that decide the route:

- Exact disposition and statutory subsection
- First-time status
- Treatment completion
- Deferred-acceptance or bail-forfeiture exception
- Statutory exclusions
