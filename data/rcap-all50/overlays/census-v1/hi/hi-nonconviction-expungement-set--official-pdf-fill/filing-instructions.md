# Filing instructions

**Route:** Nonconviction arrest expungement through the Attorney General — Haw. Rev. Stat. § 831-3.2
**Outcome mode:** agency_application — this is an application to a state agency, not a petition to a court.

## Where it goes

The form prints its own submission block:

    Submit completed application along with a self-addressed stamped envelope to:
    Hawaii Criminal Justice Data Center, Attn: Expungement
    465 South King Street, Room 102
    Honolulu, HI 96813

## What goes with it

- A copy of your valid official government-issued photo ID.
- Payment by money order or cashier's check issued in the United States, payable to "State of Hawaii". Personal checks are not accepted. The amounts printed on the form are $35 for a first-time expungement, $50 for a non-first-time expungement and $20 for a duplicate certificate; $10 of the fee is non-refundable.
- A self-addressed stamped envelope.
- Your disposition documentation.

## Service

No party is served. This is an agency application; there is no opposing party and no certificate of service.

## After it is sent

- Attorney General processing period: agency processing after the application, not a filing wait
- The form states the certificate is mailed to the address provided within 120 days, as authorized by HRS §831-3.2(a).
- The form states that because of the confidentiality of arrest records, no information — including application status — is given over the phone or by email.
