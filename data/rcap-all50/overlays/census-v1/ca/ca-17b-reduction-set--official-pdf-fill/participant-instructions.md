# Filing instructions — California offense-by-offense reduction request

This packet contains CR-180 (Petition for Dismissal), CR-181 (Order for Dismissal), and CR-106 (Proof of Service—Criminal Record Clearing). It asks for every reduction that is legally applicable to each offense under Penal Code section 17(b) or 17(d)(2). There is no single either-or choice between those sections.

> Synthetic sample: the names and case facts in the accompanying sample forms are fictional. Do not file the sample forms.

## Check the information already entered

Check your name, contact information, county, case number, conviction date, and every offense row against your court record. Each offense row needs its code, section, felony/misdemeanor/infraction classification, and a separate yes-or-no answer for each reduction column. If a row is incomplete, if the classification is unclear, or if either eligibility answer is unknown, stop and get help; do not guess and do not file a partly completed row.

## Complete CR-180

- Obtain the filing court's street address, mailing address if the court uses a different one, and city and ZIP from the Superior Court in the county of conviction.
- Complete these neutral captions on both CR-180 and CR-181: **Court street address:**, **Court mailing address:** if different, and **Court city and ZIP code:** (printed as STREET ADDRESS, MAILING ADDRESS, and CITY AND ZIP CODE).
- Select only the dismissal basis that actually applies to the case. The interests-of-justice explanations in items 2, 3, 5, and 6 are optional and belong only to the matching selected basis. If you use the human-trafficking branch in item 4, you must provide evidence that the conviction resulted from your trafficking status, in the box or in an attached letter or other relevant documents. Do not claim that branch without supporting facts. The former section 1000.3 dismissal date and related choices belong only to the deferred-entry-of-judgment branch.
- Item 4 begins, “Petitioner has completed a term of probation for a conviction under Penal Code section 647(b)”. Use it only if that completed-probation condition applies and the conviction resulted from your status as a victim of human trafficking. Its evidence requirement is separate from the optional interests-of-justice explanations.
- Read item 8 and confirm that the requested reductions match every completed offense row.
- The petitioner signs and dates CR-180 after reviewing the complete petition. The typed name is already entered; the signature and signature date remain blank for signing.

## CR-181 is for the court

The known neutral caption information is entered so the order can be matched to the case. Complete the remaining court-location caption blanks as described above; those address blanks are not judicial decisions. Do not select grant or denial choices, add findings, enter an order date, or sign for the judge. The court completes those parts.

## Serve the prosecuting attorney and complete CR-106

CR-106 covers proof of service by mail or personal delivery. For electronic service, use POS-050/EFS-050 instead. The person who serves must be at least 18. CR-106-INFO explains that the requesting participant may serve in most cases, or another adult may serve. Check the law governing your request and applicable local requirements before deciding who will serve; do not assume that every case permits participant service.

For either service method, complete CR-106's **Fill in court name and street address:** caption before filing the proof. Keep the county already entered, and obtain the court name and street address from the filing court. The county alone does not complete this box. This neutral caption task is separate from the actual server's address and service-event details.

- Choose the service method actually used. Complete only that method's section and only the recipient rows for people or agencies actually served.
- The adult server supplies their own home or business address and completes the recipient names, recipient addresses, service date, place, and time from the actual service event. LegalEase does not substitute the petitioner's address for an unknown server.
- Serve first. The adult server then types or prints their name, signs, and dates CR-106. The petitioner must not sign the server's declaration unless the petitioner is legally permitted to serve and actually did so.

For mail, the printed blanks include **Name of agency served (and person, if applicable):**, **(date):**, **from (city):**, and **(state):**. For personal delivery, they include **Name of person and agency served:** and **Date when you gave the document or form to this person:**. Complete only the applicable method and the actual recipient rows. If the deferred-entry branch applies on CR-180, also enter the **date criminal charges were dismissed under Penal Code section 1000.3:** from the court record.

The route instructions require 15 days' notice to the prosecuting attorney, but do not identify the event from which those 15 days are counted. Ask the clerk of the Superior Court in the county of conviction for the applicable timing anchor, permitted service method, and local filing sequence before service. Do not treat this packet as stating “15 days before filing.”

## Filing, fee, and copies

File CR-180 with CR-181 in the Superior Court of the county of conviction, following the clerk's local filing and service sequence. CR-106-INFO describes filing the request first and then serving the court-stamped documents in most cases; some courts instead require service first and filing the request with completed proof. Confirm which sequence applies before proceeding. After service, the actual server completes and signs CR-106; file that completed proof in the same court under its local rules. The filing fee is county-specific and no amount is stated here. Ask the clerk for the amount and accepted payment methods. If you cannot pay, ask whether California fee-waiver form FW-001 applies. Keep a complete copy of everything filed and served.

## Stop and get legal help if

- Whether the offense is a wobbler, or is listed in PC 19.8(a), is unclear from the record.
- The prosecutor objects or appears in opposition.
- The court sets a contested hearing.
- The participant is not a United States citizen.

A routine hearing does not block the initial packet. Opposition or a contested evidentiary hearing triggers handoff.

These papers do not decide whether any offense qualifies and do not represent you at an opposed or contested hearing.
