PARTICIPANT INSTRUCTIONS - MMRTA COMPLETED SENTENCE
COMPLETED SENTENCE ONLY: Form B applies after the entire sentence is complete. It is filed in the court that
sentenced you: District, Justice, Municipal or City Court as shown in your records. A person still serving the sentence
belongs on Form A, not this packet. One complete form set is required for EACH conviction, even when convictions
occurred together. Other conviction identifiers shown in the intake need separate sets; this packet reaches only its
named conviction.

PREPARE THE RECORD: obtain your Montana DOJ criminal record; Form B makes this a REQUIRED attachment.
Search BOTH Montana Public Access Portals: District Courts and Courts of Limited Jurisdiction (Searches, Case
Searches, By Party). Reconcile every discrepancy with the judgment and court record. Obtain the judgment or
sentencing order, recommended with the court petition and needed for the DOJ stage if expungement is granted.
Never treat the synthetic intake sheet as an actual official record.

LOCAL FILING AND FEES: use https://courts.mt.gov/CourtLocator/ to contact the sentencing court clerk; confirm all
local filing rules, filing method, copies, any required cover sheet and current fee before filing. The adopted May 2024
District Court civil commencement fee is $120; confirm the current amount. Justice, Municipal and City Court fees
differ: ask that court; do not use the District Court fee automatically. You are responsible for other costs, including
background records. The CRISS request has no fee.

IF UNABLE TO PAY: obtain the published Statement of Inability to Pay Court Costs and Fees at
https://www.montanalawhelp.org/resource/statement-inability-pay-court-costs-and-fees-interactive-form and follow
the clerk instructions. Its non-commercial-use restriction means this packet links to it; it does not reproduce, convert
or rewrite it. No ER-100, ER-200, DS-100 or DS-200 is reproduced.

REVIEW FORM B: confirm your identity, sentencing court, enumerated offense, conviction date and
sentence-completion date. Leave the new Case No. blank; the clerk assigns it (OCA instructions step 4). The original
conviction case number is shown in the intake for record matching. Complete the remedy election and eligibility
narrative yourself after any needed legal help. These have different consequences; this packet does not choose a
legal outcome or write your eligibility argument. Other/unlisted offenses, uncertain conduct classification or
unresolved remedy consequences require legal assistance. Select the actual attachment and hearing boxes yourself.
No hearing is necessary unless you request one; requesting a hearing is an adopted self-help STOP.

SIGN AND SERVE: the court will not consider an unsigned petition. Sign and date Form B; print your full name. No
notarization is identified on its face. Serve a copy of the petition and filed attachments upon the COUNTY
ATTORNEY FOR THE COUNTY WHERE YOU FILE. OCA Certificate of Service specifies U.S. mail and/or email:
confirm the actual recipient and address with that office before service. Complete and sign/date the certificate only
after actual service, with or before filing. The certificate is required with the petition; the generator never certifies
service or pre-fills its execution date.

FILE THE COMPLETE SET: Form B, actual DOJ criminal record, executed Certificate of Service, proposed order
and any additional confirmed attachments. The judgment is recommended. The proposed order leaves every judicial
finding, decision, hearing/opposition box, order date and judge signature blank. Only the court decides these. Keep
file-stamped copies and verify clerk acceptance. Do not file this synthetic diagnostic packet.

COURT RESPONSE: the county attorney has 21 days to respond in opposition under the OCA instructions. The
court considers the petition after a response or expiration of that period. Under MCA 16-12-113(6), the court
presumes the criteria unless the county attorney supplies a reasonable basis otherwise. Any opposition is a STOP
for legal assistance; no guarantee of relief is made. The court rules without a hearing unless you request one. Follow
any actual court notice.

AFTER AN EXPUNGEMENT ORDER: the order alone does not complete DOJ deletion. Obtain the original or
certified judgment and court expungement order. The separate CRISS unit is generated ONLY when the actual
outcome is EXPUNGEMENT and that order is received; it is not generated merely because relief was requested.
Complete the marijuana request type, review your facts and charges, sign/date the DOJ form, attach the required
original/certified court documents, and mail to Montana DOJ CRISS, 2225 11th Avenue, PO Box 201403, Helena,
MT 59620-1403. The marijuana request does NOT require an FD-258 fingerprint card; do not copy the misdemeanor
fingerprint requirement. There is no CRISS request fee. Keep copies and mailing proof and verify DOJ processing by
obtaining/checking the updated criminal record.

REDESIGNATION IS DIFFERENT: misdemeanor or civil-infraction redesignation changes classification; it does not
expunge the conviction and has no DOJ deletion request stage in this route. Do not submit a marijuana
expungement request for redesignation. Follow the signed order and confirm its correction/distribution requirements
with the court and DOJ. Resentencing concerns the currently-serving route and is not silently substituted for this
completed-sentence route.

TERMS AND LIMITS: expungement is not removal, redesignation or resentencing. DOJ expungement deletes the
specified criminal-history record; do not promise destruction of every agency/court copy or permission to deny a
conviction. Follow the order and verify any required notice/copies to the arresting agency, prosecutor and clerk; do
not stop after receiving the signed order. Whether a cleared record may lawfully be denied is a hard self-help STOP.
MMRTA relief does not disqualify a later misdemeanor-expungement petition under MCA 16-12-113(12); that is a
separate route with separate requirements.
