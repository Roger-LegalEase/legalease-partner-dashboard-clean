## When self-help must stop

Obtain legal assistance if any civil action exists or is contemplated relating to the arrest or records, including an insurance or employment dispute; if commercial driver license status at arrest is uncertain; if the offense may involve intoxication-related traffic or boating; or if chapter 303 or moving-violation classification is unclear.

Stop for legal assistance if any related offense was not nolle prossed, dismissed or acquitted; if you received a suspended imposition of sentence for this arrest or a related offense; if the prosecutor or an agency protests expungement; or if anyone appeals under section 610.124.2.

Obtain legal assistance if no local agency will provide the fingerprint cards; if you are not a United States citizen or there may be an immigration consequence; or if you need to know whether destruction means a record can never be found. The false-information ground always requires legal assistance, even if no other stop applies.
