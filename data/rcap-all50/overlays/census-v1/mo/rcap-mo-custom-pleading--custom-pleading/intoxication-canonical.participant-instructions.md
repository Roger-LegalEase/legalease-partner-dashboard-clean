## Alcohol-related enforcement contacts - section 302.525.3

For purposes of this section, "alcohol-related enforcement contacts" shall include any suspension or revocation under sections 302.500 to 302.540, any suspension or revocation entered in this or any other state for a refusal to submit to chemical testing under an implied consent law, and any conviction in this or any other state for a violation which involves driving while intoxicated, driving while under the influence of drugs or alcohol, or driving a vehicle while having an unlawful alcohol concentration.

An administrative suspension or chemical-test refusal can matter even without a criminal conviction. Compare actual Missouri and out-of-state driver records. Stop for legal help if an entry is ambiguous; this packet does not decide the legal meaning of an individual entry.
