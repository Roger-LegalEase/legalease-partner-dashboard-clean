## Packet and filing location

Prepared for Jordan Lee Example. This is a synthetic example, not an actual person or case.

This packet concerns Missouri arrest, plea, trial or conviction records under section 610.140. File in Jackson County, where every listed matter was charged or the finding of guilt entered.

Included: CR360 petition; FI-05 confidential filing sheet; the attached continuation when shown; CR370 only because this synthetic fixture says the court expects a proposed judgment; GN10 only when the participant asked for a fee-waiver form; and these instructions.

The clerk assigns the new expungement case number and filing date. The judge or clerk assigns the division. Leave all court findings, hearing information, judgment date and judge signature blank. The participant personally reviews and signs CR360. Use the planned filing date shown in the intake (09/12/2026) when checking timing; a waiting period must have run when the petition is filed.

## Verify records and participant fields before filing

Obtain a Case.net printout, certified docket sheet and judgment for every case; any probation or parole discharge; clerk payment histories showing all fines, costs and restitution satisfied; and a fingerprint-based Missouri State Highway Patrol record. Compare every case number, court, charge date, county or municipality, offense description, completion date, OCN and prior-expungement answer. Search Case.net using every current and prior name variant.

Confirm the participant identity and contact data: full legal name, date of birth, sex selection, race and ethnicity selection, current address, driver-license number/state/expiration, phone, email and full Social Security number for confidential FI-05. FI-05 is confidential and must go through the clerk's confidential filing channel.

Confirm every agency defendant against the records. An expungement order does not affect an entity that is not named. Do not invent an agency or address. Extra records, OCNs and agencies are carried on the continuation.

Review CR360 paragraphs 1 through 7 and the prior-expungement election. The official form prints sworn factual and legal statements. LegalEase has not chosen the public-safety or interests-of-justice conclusions. Sign only if every printed statement and attachment is true.

## Fees, signatures and clerk handback

Ask the filing clerk for the current county-variable filing fee, accepted payment method, copy count, and whether the court expects CR370. The former $250 surcharge was repealed and is not quoted here.

If requesting a waiver, review GN10 income, expenses, assets and debts; supply the Respondent’s Address/Telephone caption field as the clerk directs for multiple defendants; then personally date and sign the penalty-of-perjury statement. The held SJRC (07-15) GN10 is one page and carries no page-two court order. No income answer, signature or court decision is inferred.

The participant does not serve the prosecutor. The clerk gives statutory notice. A prosecutor may object in writing within thirty days after service; if there is an objection, the court may set a hearing within sixty days after the objection. Do not write a hearing date or claim that notice occurred. The court process may take up to six months for an order or dismissal; obtain the actual filed result.

After a signed judgment, obtain a filed copy and ask which named agencies received it. CR370 in this packet is an unsigned proposal and is never proof that relief was granted. Missouri relief closes records under section 610.120; it does not destroy them.

## Conviction inventory, limits and disclosures

The lifetime caps are three imprisonable misdemeanors or ordinances, two felonies, and unlimited infractions. Review whether prior relief used any cap and identify the offense level for every prior expungement. Review whether the listed matters arose from the same course of conduct; if that determination is unresolved, stop rather than grouping or separating cases by guesswork.

The six surviving disclosure situations are: (1) a Missouri professional license, certificate or permit application; (2) a Chapter 313 license or a Chapter 571 permit; (3) employment with a Chapter 313 entity, the state lottery, emergency services or law enforcement; (4) a federally insured bank, savings institution or credit union or its affiliate; (5) an insurance business or insurer; and (6) an employer that federal or state law requires to exclude certain convictions. Outside those situations, the participant may answer an employer inquiry that no public record exists as the law permits. A later court or sentencing proceeding may still require disclosure.

Confirm whether any listed case involves drug possession or paraphernalia, and review the automatic drug-relief timing before filing. Automatic relief is expected no later than January 1, 2027, but no date or result is promised. Filing a petition now may involve a fee and may use a shared lifetime slot; do not choose between routes without confirming the facts and timing.

If a prior petition was denied or withdrawn, stop and confirm the one-year refiling period measured from the date the prior petition was filed. Do not refile before that year has run.

## Stop and get legal or clerk help

Stop if any listed matter is in another filing county, if the waiting period measured from completion has not run, if obligations remain unpaid or disputed, if charges are pending, if the clean-period condition is not met, or if the agency list is incomplete.

Stop for counsel if any offense may be a class A or dangerous felony, a registration offense, a felony involving death, felony assault, domestic assault, felony kidnapping, an excluded sex offense, an intoxication-related traffic, boating or aircraft offense, an equivalent ordinance violation, a commercial-driver motor-vehicle offense, or an unlawful-use-of-weapons offense.

Stop for counsel about immigration, firearm rights, professional licensing, foster or adoption matters, banking, insurance, gaming, federal employment, or any prosecutor or victim contest. Stop if prior expungements may exhaust the lifetime cap, the course-of-conduct review is unresolved, or a denial or appeal requires litigation.

## Participant boundary

These packet pages prepare forms; they do not decide eligibility, make sworn statements for the participant, or provide a court ruling. Ask the filing clerk or counsel to resolve any question that this guide marks for a handoff.
