# Before you sign or file this packet

This packet is built on the Oregon Judicial Department's own adult set-aside
motion and declaration, and on the Oregon State Police criminal history request
that goes with it. It is a prepared draft. It is not legal advice, it is not
signed, and it has not been filed.

## The two routes in this family

- **Motion to Set Aside the Record of an Arrest, Citation or Charge Where No Accusatory Instrument Was Filed (ORS 137.225(1)(c))** - this packet marks Option 3 on page 4 of the motion, because this packet is built for ORS 137.225(1)(c), a citation or arrest where no accusatory instrument was filed and there is therefore no court case, and Option 3 is the branch the form prints for exactly that.
- **Motion to Set Aside the Record of a Dismissed Charge (ORS 137.225(1)(d))** - this packet marks Option 2 on page 4 of the motion, because this packet is built for ORS 137.225(1)(d), a dismissal in a case that reached the court, and Option 2 is the branch the form prints for a court case in which no conviction is being set aside.

Read the marked option against your own record before you file. Page 4 says to
check one option only, and a packet built on the wrong option is the wrong packet.

## Motion to Set Aside the Record of an Arrest, Citation or Charge Where No Accusatory Instrument Was Filed (ORS 137.225(1)(c))

### What you must supply before filing

- **SID# if known** - your Oregon SID number, if you know it -- it appears on Oregon State Police correspondence and on your criminal history.
- **Citing/arresting law enforcement agency** - the law enforcement agency that cited or arrested you, for example "Salem Police Dept." or "Coos County Sheriff".
- **Arrest Date** - your arrest date, or if there was no arrest the date of the citation, booking or incident.
- **Fingerprint number (FPN #) if known** - your fingerprint number (FPN #), if you know it -- the Oregon State Police assign it when a fingerprint card is processed.
- **Name of Citation/Arrest Offenses (line 1)** - the first citation or arrest offence you are asking the court to set aside, worded as it appears on the citation, the booking record or the Oregon State Police criminal history you requested.
- **Name of Citation/Arrest Offenses (line 2)** - the second citation or arrest offence you are asking the court to set aside, worded as it appears on the citation, the booking record or the Oregon State Police criminal history you requested.
- **Name of Citation/Arrest Offenses (line 3)** - the third citation or arrest offence you are asking the court to set aside, worded as it appears on the citation, the booking record or the Oregon State Police criminal history you requested.
- **Name of Citation/Arrest Offenses (line 4)** - the fourth citation or arrest offence you are asking the court to set aside, worded as it appears on the citation, the booking record or the Oregon State Police criminal history you requested.
- **Name of Citation/Arrest Offenses (line 5)** - the fifth citation or arrest offence you are asking the court to set aside, worded as it appears on the citation, the booking record or the Oregon State Police criminal history you requested.
- **Name of Citation/Arrest Offenses (line 6)** - the sixth citation or arrest offence you are asking the court to set aside, worded as it appears on the citation, the booking record or the Oregon State Police criminal history you requested.
- **Name of Citation/Arrest Offenses (line 7)** - the seventh citation or arrest offence you are asking the court to set aside, worded as it appears on the citation, the booking record or the Oregon State Police criminal history you requested.
- **Name of Citation/Arrest Offenses (line 8)** - the eighth citation or arrest offence you are asking the court to set aside, worded as it appears on the citation, the booking record or the Oregon State Police criminal history you requested.
- **Phone** - your phone number, written by hand in the Phone column at the right end of the address line -- the court's printed column is 34 points wide and no phone number fits it at the minimum readable machine font, so this packet leaves that column to your pen.
- **The Oregon State Police Request for Set Aside Criminal Record Check, completed in full by the participant** - every entry on the Oregon State Police request form, which you send to the Oregon State Police with your fingerprint card before you file the motion.

### What only you can answer

These are your sworn statements about your own case. Nothing here marks them.
Read each one and tick the ones that are true of you:

- I have waited the required period under law to file this Motion
- I believe I am legally eligible for a set aside
- I have filed fingerprints with the Oregon State Police
- I will serve a copy of this Motion on the prosecuting attorney

### What is left unmarked because this route does not use it

- Option 1
- Option 2
- I am not currently charged with a crime or contempt of court related to abuse or a person crime
- I have paid the Oregon State Police background check fee
- I have fully complied and performed all terms of the sentence of the court

## What is deliberately left blank on every fixture

Your signature and the date beside it are blank on the declaration and on the
certificate of mailing, and so is your printed name on the certificate. A
certificate saying a copy was mailed on a date when it had not been mailed would
be false. Complete the certificate at the time you actually mail the copy.

The Phone column at the right of the address line is left to your pen. The
court's printed column is 34 points wide and no phone number fits it at the
smallest readable machine font, so this packet does not try.

## There is no proposed order in this packet

The packet contains the Motion, the Declaration of Eligibility, the Certificate
of Mailing and the Oregon State Police criminal history request. It contains no
proposed order. The court's own packet does not include one, the Oregon corpus
holds none, and nothing here drafts an order for a judge to sign.
