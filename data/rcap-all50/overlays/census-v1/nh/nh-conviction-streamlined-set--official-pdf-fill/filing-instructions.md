**Post-order State Police fee.** After a successful conviction annulment, expect a separate $100 Department of Safety/State Police record-correction charge, subject to any applicable agency waiver or exemption and the agency's actual assessment. **Notice.** The court submits a notice of its determination to the person convicted and to the prosecutor. The prosecutor has 20 days from receipt to object, in writing, and only on the ground that the offence is not eligible or that all terms and conditions of the sentence are not complete.
