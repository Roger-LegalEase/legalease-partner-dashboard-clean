**Where.** File NHJB-3124-DS with the clerk of the court of conviction or arrest, and furnish a copy to the office of the prosecutor of the underlying offence.

The destination is the Clerk of the court of conviction or arrest. Filed with the court, with a copy furnished by the petitioner to the office of the prosecutor of the underlying offence. This differs from RSA 651:5, IX, where the court provides the copy, and the difference is a step the packet must build in.

**Venue.** The court in which the person was convicted or arrested. That is broader than the general rule and lets an arrest-only participant file where the arrest occurred.

**Fees.** Not addressed by RSA 651:5-b, which unlike RSA 651:5-a contains no fee exemption. Whether the $125.00 filing fee and the $100 Department of Corrections investigation fee apply is recorded below as an open question, so the packet must not state a price.

**Waiver.** Motion for Waiver of Filing Fee, NHJB-2311, with the Statement of Assets and Liabilities, NHJB-2328, where a fee is charged.

**Service — by the petitioner on this route.** By the petitioner, not the court. This differs from the general rule under RSA 651:5, IX.

**Notice.** The petitioner furnishes a copy of the petition to the office of the prosecutor of the underlying offence. The prosecutor may object within 10 days of receipt and request a hearing.

**Signature.** Sworn signature on the petition.

**Open questions the record has not settled, and which this packet does not answer:**
