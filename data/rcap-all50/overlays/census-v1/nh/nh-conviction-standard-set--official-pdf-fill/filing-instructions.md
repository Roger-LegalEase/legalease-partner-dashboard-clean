**Which petition.** File one, chosen by the date of conviction:

- NHJB-2317-DSe — Where the conviction date is before January 1, 2019.
- NHJB-3057-DSe — Where the conviction date is on or after January 1, 2019 and the offence was not the highest offense of conviction, so the streamlined mandatory route does not apply.

**Where.** File the correct petition variant with the clerk of the court that disposed of the charge, one petition per charge. The variant is chosen by conviction date at the January 1, 2019 line.

The destination is the Clerk of the disposing court. Unified statewide system with no local variation identified. One petition per charge; petitions filed at the same time in one court location share a single $125.00 filing fee. The court orders a Department of Corrections investigation, which the petitioner pays for, and provides a copy of the petition to the prosecutor.

**Venue.** The Circuit Court District Division or Superior Court that disposed of the charge. Separate petitions for charges in different courts.

**Fees.** $125.00 per court location, confirmed on the current Circuit Court Filing Fees schedule and covering petitions filed at the same time in that court. $100 Department of Corrections investigation fee under RSA 651:5, IX unless the petitioner demonstrates indigency. $100 Department of Safety fee for a successful petitioner. State police fee not to exceed $100 under X(d). Whether the Department of Safety fee and the state police fee are one charge or two is recorded below.

**Waiver.** Motion for Waiver of Filing Fee, NHJB-2311, with the Statement of Assets and Liabilities, NHJB-2328. Indigency exempts the Department of Corrections and Department of Safety fees under RSA 651:5, IX. The state police fee waiver request must be accompanied by an affidavit of the petitioner's and spouse's monthly net income and assets.

**Service.** None by the participant.

**Notice.** The court provides a copy of the petition to the prosecutor of the underlying offence under RSA 651:5, IX. No objection period is specified for this route; the prosecutor is permitted to be heard on the interest of justice.

**Signature.** Sworn signature on the petition under penalties of law.

**Notarization.** None identified.
