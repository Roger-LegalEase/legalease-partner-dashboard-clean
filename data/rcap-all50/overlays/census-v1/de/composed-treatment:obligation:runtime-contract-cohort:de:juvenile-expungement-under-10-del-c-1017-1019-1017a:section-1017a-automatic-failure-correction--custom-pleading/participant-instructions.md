# What you must do before you file — getting a Delaware § 1017A record expunged where the automatic programme has not reached it

This packet is prepared for **Delaware § 1017A automatic juvenile expungement — request and guidance where the automatic process did not occur**.

**This packet contains no court petition, and that is deliberate.** The committed records describe the Delaware § 1017A juvenile route as AUTOMATIC: eligible cases are expunged by the State Bureau of Identification monthly, with no filing. This packet is for the case where that has not happened.

## What the committed record says you must know

Each of these is carried here in the words of the committed record it comes from, because a participant who does not know it may file the wrong thing, or file something they did not need to file at all.

- **The compiled Delaware profile** — None (automatic, § 4373A) Mandatory-eligible cases are expunged by the SBI monthly with no filing.
- **The compiled Delaware profile** — SBI mandatory-expungement request Contact the SBI (302-739-5884) to request mandatory expungement; if eligible, SBI sends a letter with instructions and any fees.
- **The committed route contract** — § 1017 favorable-termination matters are mandatory with no elapsed wait; § 1017A eligible records run through the automatic program with a petition or correction path only where the automatic process did not occur. The three-year value here is the lowest discretionary § 1018 branch; the five- and seven-year branches turn on the offense category, which the intake must collect.

## What you do, in order

1. **Check whether the record is still there.** If the automatic programme has already reached it, there is nothing to ask for.
2. **If it is still there, send the request page, or telephone the SBI on 302-739-5884.** The compiled record says that if you are eligible, the SBI sends a letter with instructions and any fees.
3. **Do not file a court petition first.** The committed records describe a petition or correction path only where the automatic process did not occur.
4. **Keep everything you send and everything you get back.**
5. **If the answer does not resolve it, take it to a lawyer or a legal-aid office.** That is where an agency question becomes a legal one.

## Things the platform deliberately left blank

- **Your signing line, and the date beside it.** A signature is yours alone, and a date written before you sign would be false.
- **Any court caption.** There is none, because nothing here is filed in a court.

## What this packet does not tell you

- **Whether any fee attaches to an SBI mandatory-expungement request.** The compiled record says the SBI's letter states any fees; it does not state what they are. The SBI, on 302-739-5884, is the office that answers it.

## When to stop and get help instead of filing

- the SBI says the record is not eligible and you believe it is;
- the SBI does not answer, or answers and nothing changes;
- you are told a court petition is now needed;
- the record belongs to the § 1017 mandatory branch or the § 1018 discretionary branch instead;
- any immigration question is involved.

## What this packet is not

This is a request to an agency and a page of guidance. It is not a court petition — the committed records describe this branch as automatic, and no committed record names a form for it. It is not legal advice, it is not sent for you, and it does not make the State Bureau of Identification act.
