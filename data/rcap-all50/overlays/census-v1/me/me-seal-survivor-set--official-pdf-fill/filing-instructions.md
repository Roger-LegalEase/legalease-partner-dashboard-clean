# Filing your Maine survivor sealing motion

## Where it goes

File the completed CR-307 with the proposed CR-308 order at the clerk's office for the court
that entered the conviction, in the underlying criminal proceeding (15 M.R.S. § 2264(1)). Where
several motions are filed, the court consolidates them to one location.

## The filing fee

The committed record says no fee is expected: Administrative Order JB-05-26 lists no fee for a
criminal post-judgment motion. This packet states no fee amount. Ask the clerk.

## Service

The committed record says § 2264(3) directs notice to the prosecutorial office that represented
the State, and that no service obligation is imposed on the movant by the statute or by CR-307 —
the form carries no certificate-of-service block. Whether you serve the prosecutor or the court
performs all notice, and by what method, is not settled in the committed record for this route.
Ask the clerk what that court requires.

## The hearing

Section 2264(5) requires a hearing on a motion under the chapter, except that for a motion under
§ 2262-B the court may grant the motion without a hearing where the State consents. CR-308's
first ordering box is the no-hearing route.

If the State opposes, or the court sets a contested hearing, automated assistance ends and you
need a lawyer.
