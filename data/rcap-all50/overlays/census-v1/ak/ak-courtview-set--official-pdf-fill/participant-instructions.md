# Filing instructions — Getting a closed case off the public court index

This packet is the Alaska Court System's form **TF-810**, _Request to Exclude Case from Online Public Index (CourtView) Under Administrative Rule 40(a) or AS 22.35.030_, prepared under AS 22.35.030; Alaska Administrative Rule 40(a); Alaska Court System form TF-810 (5/25).

## What this form is, and what it is not

It is a **correction request**, not a petition. The form says so on its own face: the ten grounds listed on it are removals **the court system performs automatically**, and this form is for a requester who believes the court made an error and their case should already have come off the online public index (CourtView). There is no judge's order on it and nothing to argue — you tick the ground that is true of your case and file it at your local trial court.

The form routes two neighbouring requests away from itself, and it is right to:

- To ask a judge to make a **still-public case, or records inside it, confidential or sealed**, use **TF-800**.
- To remove **only your name** rather than the whole case, use **TF-805**.

## Where you file this

File it with the **clerk's office of your local Alaska trial court** — the court named in the case number at the top of the form. The form says so in its own words.

**Any filing fee is not stated here**, because it is not established in any source this packet holds and an unsourced figure in a filing instruction is worse than none. Ask the clerk.

## The two grounds that need an attachment

- **Ground 4** (a suspended imposition of sentence): the form tells you to attach proof of the SIS and any order setting aside.
- **Ground 9** (already confidential or sealed under Administrative Rule 37.6): the form tells you to attach a copy of that order.

The platform holds neither attachment and does not attach them for you.

## What you must do before you file

1. **Fill in the items in the table below.**
2. **Tick the one ground that is true of your case**, from the list under _The choices that are yours_. If you tick ground 10, write the rule or statute number on the line beneath it.
3. **Attach what grounds 4 and 9 require**, if you ticked either.
4. **Sign and date the form yourself.** Both lines are left blank on purpose.

## The items you must supply

| Page | The blank on the form | What to write |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Case Name | the case name exactly as it appears on your court paperwork — in a criminal case that is usually the State of Alaska against your name |
| 1 | Ground 10 — the specific rule or statute number | the specific Alaska statute or court rule number that takes your case off the public index, if you ticked ground 10 |

## The choices that are yours

| Page | The choice | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ground 1 — 60 days have passed since acquittal or dismissal (selection) | tick this if you were acquitted of all charges, or all charges were dismissed and the dismissal was not part of a plea agreement in another criminal case, or a combination of the two — and 60 days have passed |
| 1 | Ground 2 — a criminal case dismissed for one of the two reasons below (selection) | tick this heading if one of the two reasons printed under it is why your case was dismissed |
| 1 | Ground 2 — a judicial officer found no probable cause (selection) | tick this if a judicial officer found no probable cause under Criminal Rule 4(a)(1) or 5(d) |
| 1 | Ground 2 — the defendant was a minor wrongly charged in adult court (selection) | tick this if the defendant was a minor and was wrongly charged in adult court with an offence within the jurisdiction for delinquency proceedings under AS 47.12.020 |
| 1 | Ground 3 — a criminal or minor offence case dismissed for one of the two reasons below (selection) | tick this heading if one of the two reasons printed under it is why your case was dismissed |
| 1 | Ground 3 — the prosecutor decided not to file a charging document (selection) | tick this if the prosecutor decided not to file a charging document |
| 1 | Ground 3 — there was an identity error (selection) | tick this if there was an identity error under Criminal Rule 43(d) or Minor Offense Rule 11(c) |
| 1 | Ground 4 — a suspended imposition of sentence, completed, with all other charges resolved (selection) | tick this if you received a suspended imposition of sentence, completed the terms of the sentence, and were acquitted of — or had dismissed or set aside — all other charges in the same case. | 1 | Ground 5 — all charges were underage alcohol offences (selection) | tick this if every charge was one of the underage alcohol offences the form lists — AS 04.16.049, AS 04.16.050, AS 04.16.060(g), AS 28.35.280, AS 28.35.285 or AS 28.35.290 — or an equivalent municipal ordinance |
| 1 | Ground 6 — a conviction for possessing less than one ounce of marijuana at 21 or older (selection) | tick this if you were convicted of possessing less than one ounce of marijuana, were at least 21 at the time of the offence, and were not convicted of any other criminal charge in the same case |

## What the platform deliberately left blank

- **Your signature and its date.**
- **Every ground.** Each is a statement about how your case ended, and only you know which is true.
- **The clerk's block at the foot of the form** — the response, the date sent and the initials. That is the court's.

## What this packet is not

This is a prepared copy of an official Alaska Court System form. It is not legal advice, it is not filed for you, and it does not decide whether your case comes off the public index.
