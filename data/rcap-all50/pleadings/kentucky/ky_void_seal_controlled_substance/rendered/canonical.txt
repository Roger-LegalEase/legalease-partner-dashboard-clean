STATUTORY AUTHORITY

KRS 218A.275(8) — After satisfactory completion of treatment, probation or other sentence on a first conviction for possession of a controlled substance, the court may void the conviction.
KRS 218A.275(9) — On voiding, the court shall order the records sealed, except as provided in KRS 27A.099.
KRS 218A.275(10) — A voided conviction is not a first offence for later Chapter 218A purposes and is not a conviction for disqualifications or disabilities imposed by law.
KRS 218A.275(12) — Voiding may occur only once for any person, and the subsection excludes a person who has previously had a charge dismissed after completion of a deferred prosecution programme under KRS 218A.14151.
KRS 27A.099 — Records excepted from sealing. Records excepted by KRS 27A.099 are not sealed.
KRS 431.078(2) — The five-year period does not apply where the conviction was voided under KRS 218A.275(8). Carried as a packet instruction for onward routing, not as relief requested in this motion.
