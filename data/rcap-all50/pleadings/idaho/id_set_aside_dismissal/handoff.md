## Mechanism

Where sentence was imposed but suspended, or was withheld, on the defendant's
application and a **satisfactory showing of compliance with probation**, or
graduation from an authorized drug court or mental health court program, the
court may terminate the sentence or set aside the plea or conviction and finally
dismiss the case and discharge the defendant.

Final dismissal **restores civil rights**. It does **not** hide the record, and it
is **not** an expungement. Venue is the sentencing court.

An offense requiring sex offender registration under I.C. § 18-8304 is not
subject to dismissal or reduction under this section. "Conviction" here includes
a plea or finding of guilt notwithstanding the form of the judgment or a withheld
judgment.
