STATUTORY AUTHORITY

O.C.G.A. § 42-8-62.1(c) — An individual who was sentenced pursuant to Article 3 of Chapter 8 of Title 42 prior to July 1, 2026 and has not had their sentence revoked and adjudicated guilty may petition the court that ordered such sentence to limit public access to his or her case information pursuant to subsection (b). Notice of the petition shall be sent to the clerk of court and the prosecuting attorney, and notice sent by registered or certified mail or statutory overnight delivery is sufficient.
O.C.G.A. § 42-8-62.1(d) — Within 90 days of the filing the court shall order restriction and sealing. The former requirement that the court find by a preponderance of the evidence that a discharge had been granted and that privacy harm outweighed the public interest was struck, so relief on a properly filed petition is mandatory and no balancing showing is required.
O.C.G.A. § 42-8-62.1(e) — The clerk seals every document in its custody within 60 days of the court's order.
O.C.G.A. § 42-8-62.1(f) — Law enforcement agencies, jails and detention centers must restrict within 30 days of receiving a copy of the order.
