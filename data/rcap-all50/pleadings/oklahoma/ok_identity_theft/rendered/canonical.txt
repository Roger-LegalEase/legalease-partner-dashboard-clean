STATUTORY AUTHORITY

22 O.S. § 19a — Identity-theft / wrong-person expungement under 22 O.S. § 19a and the Section 18 identity-theft category: available where the person was charged, arrested, or subject to a warrant for a crime committed by another person who used their name or identifying information without consent.
22 O.S. §§ 18-19 — Section 18/19 adult expungement under 22 O.S. §§ 18-19 covers categories including acquittal, conviction reversed and dismissed, DNA factual innocence, arrest with no charges filed, all charges dismissed without a deferred judgment, deferred/delayed-sentence dismissals, eligible misdemeanor and felony convictions, a nonviolent felony reclassified as a misdemeanor, full gubernatorial pardons, and identity-theft / wrong-person records.
22 O.S. § 19 — Section 19 procedure under 22 O.S. § 19: the qualified person petitions the district court where the arrest information is located; the court sets a hearing with 30 days' notice to the prosecuting agency, arresting agency, OSBI, and any other relevant agency, and may seal records if the harm to privacy or danger of unwarranted adverse consequences outweighs the public interest in retaining them. After sealing, the official actions are deemed never to have occurred. Section 19 does not authorize physical destruction of criminal-justice records.
