STATUTORY AUTHORITY

20 ILCS 2630/5.2(g) — Immediate sealing. Arrests or charges not initiated by arrest resulting in acquittal or dismissal with prejudice on or after January 1, 2018 may be sealed immediately, on a petition filed with the circuit clerk on the same day and during the same hearing in which the case is disposed.
20 ILCS 2630/5.2(g)(5)(A) — Frames the filing as made by the defendant's attorney on the defendant's behalf: "the defendant's attorney may immediately petition the court, on behalf of the defendant." This is the provision that makes the route attorney-mediated.
