STATUTORY AUTHORITY

HRS § 706-622.9 — Conviction expungement for a first-time property offender. The grant is mandatory once the statutory findings are made — 'shall issue ... provided that (a) through (d)' — but two of the four provisos are findings the court makes, so the outcome is not mechanical; where the court cannot make the paragraph (b) finding it may nevertheless order expungement on finding successful completion of a substance abuse treatment program. This relief must not be described as automatic.
Haw. R. Penal P. 47(a) — 'An application to the court for an order shall be by motion' — the stage-one vehicle is a written motion in the participant's own existing penal case, filed in the court that sentenced or convicted them; the Rules of Penal Procedure govern because Rule 54(a) applies them to all penal proceedings.
