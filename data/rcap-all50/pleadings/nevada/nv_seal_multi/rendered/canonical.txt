STATUTORY AUTHORITY

NRS 179.2595 — A person seeking to seal more than one record may petition the district court for the county, which may seal all the records within that county including justice and municipal court cases. This is a venue overlay on the conviction and non-conviction sealing sections rather than a separate remedy: each underlying record must still satisfy its own section.
NRS 179.245 — Conviction sealing. A verified criminal history from the Nevada Central Repository is a statutory attachment under NRS 179.245(2)(a). Eligibility turns on the offence category — category A through E felony, gross misdemeanour or misdemeanour — and on whether the offence is a crime of violence.
NRS 179.255 — Sealing where the proceedings did not result in a conviction. A verified criminal history from the Nevada Central Repository is a statutory attachment under NRS 179.255(3)(a).
NRS 179.285 — On sealing, the proceedings are deemed never to have occurred and civil rights are restored. NRS 179.295 allows sealed records to be reopened and NRS 179.301 allows certain persons and agencies to inspect them.
