## Mechanism

NRS 179.271, "Sealing of records after decriminalization of offense", provides a
route where the conduct underlying a Nevada record is no longer a crime. The
controlling review records the instrument as a **written request** rather than a
full petition, and classified the track as `custom_pleading` on that basis.

The section's presence and official title were confirmed in the chapter index at
the Nevada Legislature on 2026-08-06. **Its text was not reproduced in the
review.**

Under NRS 179.285 the proceedings are deemed never to have occurred and civil
rights are restored; NRS 179.295 allows sealed records to be reopened and NRS
179.301 allows certain persons and agencies to inspect them.
