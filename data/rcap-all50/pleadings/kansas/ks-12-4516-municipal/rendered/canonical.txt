PETITION FOR EXPUNGEMENT OF A CITY ORDINANCE CONVICTION OR DIVERSION PURSUANT TO K.S.A. 12-4516

STATUTORY AUTHORITY

K.S.A. 12-4516 — A person convicted of a violation of a city ordinance may petition the convicting municipal court to expunge the conviction and related arrest records; a person who has fulfilled the terms of a diversion agreement based on a city ordinance violation may likewise petition the court. The court sets a hearing and causes notice to be given to the prosecuting attorney and the arresting law enforcement agency, and shall order expungement on three findings: that the petitioner has not been convicted of a felony in the past two years and no such proceeding is pending or being instituted; that the petitioner's circumstances and behaviour warrant expungement; and that expungement is consistent with the public welfare. There is no firearms finding, because a municipal ordinance conviction is not a felony.
K.S.A. 12-4516(a)(1) — Conviction of a city ordinance violation: the petition may be brought once three or more years have elapsed from the latest of satisfying the sentence imposed or discharge from probation, parole or a suspended sentence.
K.S.A. 12-4516(a)(2) — Fulfilled diversion agreement based on a city ordinance violation: the petition may be brought once three or more years have elapsed from the date the terms of the diversion agreement were fulfilled.
K.S.A. 12-4516(b) — An ordinance violation prohibited by K.S.A. 12-16,134(a) or (b) and adopted before July 1, 2014: no elapsed period is stated for this category.
K.S.A. 12-4516(c) — An ordinance violation that would also constitute prostitution under K.S.A. 21-6419 or former K.S.A. 21-3512, where the petitioner can prove coercion caused by the act of another: one year from satisfying the sentence, fulfilling the diversion terms or discharge from supervision.
K.S.A. 12-4516(d) — Five years for an ordinance violation that would also constitute vehicular homicide, driving while cancelled, suspended or revoked, perjury under K.S.A. 8-261a, a fraudulent application under K.S.A. 8-142 Fifth, a felony in which a motor vehicle was used, failing to stop at an accident scene, no motor vehicle liability insurance under K.S.A. 40-3104, or a violation of former K.S.A. 21-3405b.
K.S.A. 12-4516(e) — Five years for an ordinance violation that would also constitute a first violation of K.S.A. 8-1567 (driving under the influence) under (e)(1), and ten years for a second or subsequent violation under (e)(2). Subsection (e) applies to all violations committed on or after July 1, 2006, and the municipal statute has no equivalent of the K.S.A. 21-6614(d)(3) seven-year window.
K.S.A. 12-16,134 — Identifies the ordinance categories referenced by K.S.A. 12-4516(b), and is carried in the pinned registry authority list for this track.
