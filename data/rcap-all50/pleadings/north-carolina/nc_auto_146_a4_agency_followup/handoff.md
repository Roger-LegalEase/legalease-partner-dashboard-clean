# Handoff — nc_auto_146_a4_agency_followup (Lane C2)

## Authority

N.C. Gen. Stat. §§ 15A-146(a4), 15A-150, 15A-151(a1), 15A-153 (pinned registry authority list). Track is `missing_from_compiled_runtime`; the pinned registry entry is the operative source.

## Mechanism

Post-relief supporting action: the participant's own signed written request asking a non-court record holder (arresting agency, state agency, SBI) to conform its records to an automatic expunction that has already occurred under § 15A-146(a4). Seeks no new relief; asks no court for anything.

## Route decision — correspondence form

Rendered via the lane C2 correspondence composer (`documentForm: "correspondence"`), per the registry's "This is correspondence, not a filing." The letter asks-and-does-not-demand: the registry's open counsel question on whether any conformity duty exists means the request is framed as check-and-conform, never as an accusation of breached duty, and it never certifies the expunction.

## Open counsel flags

1. Registry counsel question: is AOC-G-260 the correct instrument for evidencing an automatic § 15A-146(a4) expunction to a non-court holder, and if not, what is?
2. Registry counsel question: does any NC statute or rule oblige a non-court record holder to conform its records on notice of an automatic expunction? (Letter asks, does not demand, pending resolution.)
3. Timing gate: outer edge at least 210 days from final disposition; inside 180–210-day window the route has not matured.
4. Exclusions: federal/out-of-state holders; the court record itself (§ 15A-151(a1)); cases where the automatic expunction did not run (petition tracks apply).
5. Immigration caution and refuse/non-response handoff are hard self-help boundaries.
