# How to Complete and File Your Tennessee Petition to Clear an Arrest the Court Has No Record Of

These instructions are for you, the person filing. **Do not file this page with the court** — it is a checklist, not a court document.

## What this packet is

This packet is a petition for the unusual case where an **arrest record exists on your criminal history but the court has no history of the arrest in its own records** — your arrest never produced a court file.

This is the one Tennessee route where **you** write the operative petition yourself. On the routes that clear a conviction, the district attorney general's office has to prepare the petition. Here it does not: the section that imposes that duty does not apply to this one.

## Read this first

**Nothing here is automatic.** A charge that was dismissed, and a diversion you completed, stay on background checks until somebody files a petition. This is the single most common Tennessee misunderstanding, and it is why this packet exists.

**There is no official Tennessee form for any of this.** Both state form indices were checked directly and neither publishes an expunction form of any kind. What varies locally is the clerk's own paperwork, so ask your clerk before you file.

**Check the section number before you file.** The chapter was renumbered in 2025 and again on codification in 2026, and clerks and published guidance may still be using the old numbers — the state's own expunction page still reasons from a section that has been nothing but definitions since April 2025. Your packet carries the current citation. Confirm it with the clerk or the district attorney general's office before you file, because a petition citing the wrong section can be rejected either way.

## This route is not free, and there is no waiver

The section makes the clerk's fee **mandatory** and contains no indigency provision, no waiver and no without-cost language — in express contrast to the free route for dismissed charges. The amount is set by a separate fee statute that is not published on any official state site, so this packet quotes no figure. **Ask the clerk what it costs before you commit to this route.**

**Make sure you are on the right route first.** If your case did reach the court and was dismissed, no-true-billed, nolle prossed or ended in an acquittal, you belong on the **free** route instead. Do not pay for this one if that free route is open to you.

## Before you file

1. **Confirm from your own criminal history that an arrest record actually exists.** If you cannot establish that, go back to the state bureau before doing anything else.
2. **Confirm you were never arraigned and never appeared** in that court on the arrest, and that you know of no court file.
3. **Get your state control number early.** It comes from your own criminal history, and the order cannot be completed without it. Fill it into the bracket before you file.
4. **Gather anything you want the court to see.** You may file evidence, and so may the district attorney general. Nothing in this packet is generated as evidence.

- **Leave the court's blanks alone.** The judge's name, any hearing date and the case-number stamp are supplied by the court after you file, not by you.
- **Fill in every item shown in [BRACKETS]** from your own records before you file.
- **You sign it.** Notarisation is not required by the governing section, but county practice can differ — ask your clerk whether they want it notarised.

## Filing, service and what happens next

- The clerk serves the petition on the district attorney general. **You do not serve anybody.**
- **The clerk searches the court's records and certifies to the court** whether there is any history of the arrest. That certification is the clerk's own document. You do not prepare it and neither does this packet.
- The court reviews the clerk's certification and the evidence, and may enter the order if it finds no history of the arrest in the court's record.
- No certificate from the state bureau is required on this route — the court is expressly exempted.
- There is **no** waiting period, no 61-day pre-order clock and no two-year refiling bar on this route. All of those belong to a different section.

## What Tennessee expunction does not reach

It does not reach federal, military, tribal or out-of-state records. If you have a security clearance question or an immigration matter, get legal advice before you file anything.

## When to get a lawyer

Stop and talk to an attorney or legal aid if the court does in fact have a record of the arrest — that takes you to the free route instead; the district attorney general files evidence that the court does have a history of the arrest; whether a court file ever existed is disputed; you cannot establish from your criminal history that an arrest record exists at all; or you have federal, military, tribal or out-of-state records, an immigration matter or a security-clearance question.
