## Mechanism

It is resolved: Public Chapter 268 of 2025 created a partial database removal provision at T.C.A. § 40-32-106(c)(2), and the TBI's own form BI-0334, revision 04/2025, is captioned Removal of Criminal Offender Record pursuant to T.C.A. § 40-32-106(c)(2), which corroborates both the citation and the mechanism from an official source. The remedy answers the most common real-world Tennessee scenario after a clean dismissal: a multi-count case where some counts were dismissed as part of a plea and others produced a conviction. ## Open counsel flags

1. Expunction is not automatic. A dismissed charge or a completed diversion stays on background checks until a petition is filed. This is the most common Tennessee misunderstanding and the packet leads with it.
2. No official Tennessee participant form exists. Both state form indices were inspected directly and neither publishes an expunction form of any kind; this is a negative finding established from the authoritative index, not a failure to search. A controlled custom instrument is therefore the only available strategy, and it is not represented as an official form.
3. Chapter 32 was reorganized by Public Chapter 268 of 2025 and codified by Public Chapter 608 effective 2026-03-25, and clerks and published guidance may still use the pre-reorganization numbering — the state's own expunction page still reasons from a section that has been nothing but definitions since 2025-04-24. A petition citing the wrong section is a rejection risk either way, so the packet carries the current citation and instructs the participant to confirm it with the clerk or the district attorney general before filing.
4. Tennessee expunction reaches no federal, military, tribal or out-of-state record, and a diversion guilty plea may remain visible on a clearance check.
5. The governing section prescribes no caption form and no contents for the participant's instrument. The caption follows the ordinary criminal-case style of the court having jurisdiction, with the filer designated as the source designates them. What individual clerks and courts in fact expect is not established and no county practice was surveyed; the caption is a visual- and counsel-review item.
6. This is partial removal, not expunction of the case. The counts that produced the conviction are untouched and are eligible only through the conviction analysis, if at all. A participant who expects the whole case cleared is a stop condition.
7. Whether the relief reaches paper records or only electronic databases was not established, and the source flags that limitation as materially changing what a participant gets. The packet withholds any assertion about paper coverage. A participant who needs the paper file cleared is a stop condition.
8. Whether any fee applies to a partial removal was not confirmed. The no-cost provision is framed around the non-conviction grounds and whether it reaches a partial removal in a mixed case is unresolved. No figure is quoted.
9. The count-by-count disposition schedule is a manual-completion item taken from the clerk's record. Where the dispositions cannot be established from that record, self-help stops.
10. The state control number is a manual-completion item read from the participant's own criminal history. It stays a bracket in generated output; the order cannot be completed without it, and the participant is told to capture it early.
11. The shared renderer's fixed section heading "VI. VERIFICATION" is a Pennsylvania-derived artefact of the frozen renderer contract, not a Tennessee drafting choice. Nothing in the governing section requires verification. This is a visual- and counsel-review item.
12. No fee figure is quoted anywhere in this packet. The source records no retrievable amount, and the filing-fee field is left null rather than filled with a circulating number.
13. No fee-waiver instrument is included and the fee-waiver field is left null, because the source establishes no waiver mechanism for this route.
14. The governing section states no waiting period. None is pleaded, none is imported from a neighbouring section, and the waiting-period field is left null.
15. Notarization is not prescribed by the governing section and no verification statute exists to cite, so verificationStatute.citation is left null and the signature block carries no penalty citation. County practice may differ, and the packet directs the participant to ask the clerk.
