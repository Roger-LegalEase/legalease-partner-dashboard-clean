STATUTORY AUTHORITY

T.C.A. § 40-32-106(d)(1) — On successful completion of a pretrial diversion programme the charges are dismissed, and the person may then petition for expunction of the public records where the charges were dismissed as a result of that successful completion. A petition is required; expunction is not automatic.
T.C.A. § 40-32-106(d)(2) — Bars expunction where the diverted offence was a sexual offence or a violent sexual offence as defined by the registration statute.
T.C.A. § 40-32-106(d)(3) — Requires the appropriate clerk's fee, so unlike a straight dismissal this route is not free.
T.C.A. §§ 40-15-102 through 40-15-107 — The pretrial diversion scheme under which prosecution is suspended by memorandum of understanding with the district attorney general; the person does not plead guilty and is not found guilty.
T.C.A. § 40-32-102(c) — The certificate requirement, which applies on this route because the exemption for expunctions under the non-conviction section is carved back where the expunction resulted from successful completion of diversion.
