## What the source does settle

The authority, the eligible population and the trigger. A person who was 24 or younger at the time of the offense may move to set aside the conviction after completing probation, incarceration, supervised release or parole, whichever is later, regardless of whether they were sentenced under the Act. The relief is discretionary: the court considers the statutory factors and makes a written statement on the record of its reasons. Venue is the Superior Court's Criminal Division, one motion per case number. A certificate issues where the conviction is set aside.

## What a reviewer should check first

Before anything else, check whether the participant already has the relief without a motion. The pin records that an unconditional discharge of a committed youth offender before expiration of the sentence **automatically** sets aside the conviction, and that automatic set-aside follows early termination of supervised release by the Parole Commission. A participant in either position needs no motion at all.

Second: a set-aside is **not** sealing. Even once this route unblocks, the associated arrest and case records need their own sealing motion — and whether the two are filed together or in sequence is one of the open questions above.
