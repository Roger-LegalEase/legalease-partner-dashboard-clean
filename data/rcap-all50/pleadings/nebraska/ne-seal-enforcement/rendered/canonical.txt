STATUTORY AUTHORITY

Neb. Rev. Stat. § 29-3528 — Where any officer or employee of the state, its agencies or its political subdivisions, or any state agency or political subdivision, fails to comply with the Security, Privacy, and Dissemination of Criminal History Information Act, any person aggrieved may bring an action, including but not limited to an action for mandamus, to compel compliance. Venue is statutory: the district court of any district in which the records involved are located, or the district court of Lancaster County.
Neb. Rev. Stat. § 29-3523(3) — Supplies the underlying sealing duty on which this action rests. The action becomes available where the applicable § 29-3523(3) period has run or its trigger has occurred and the record is still public.
Neb. Rev. Stat. § 29-3523(7) — Carried in the pinned registry authority list for this track as part of the § 29-3523 sealing scheme.
Neb. Rev. Stat. § 29-3527 — Carried in the pinned registry authority list for this track as part of the Security, Privacy, and Dissemination of Criminal History Information Act.
State v. Coble, 299 Neb. 434 (2018) — Identifies the § 29-3528 action as the correct route, in contrast to a motion filed in the criminal case.
