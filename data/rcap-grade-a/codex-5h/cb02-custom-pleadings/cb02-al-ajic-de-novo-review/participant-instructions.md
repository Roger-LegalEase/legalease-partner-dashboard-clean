# Participant instructions

Do not file this candidate fixture. Confirm every checklist item and obtain the locally required cover sheet. Stop and seek professional help if any of these applies:
- the agency disputes a material fact concerning identity, disposition, release or clearance
- the agency contends that § 41-9-625 does not require removal of the particular information
- a motion to dismiss or jurisdictional challenge is filed
- discovery, subpoenas, witness preparation, live evidentiary testimony or cross-examination is required
- bespoke legal briefing is required
- appellate review beyond the circuit court is sought
- the participant is actually seeking a general Alabama expungement under Title 15 rather than correction or removal of AJIC criminal-history information
- the requested relief includes sealing or erasing court files
