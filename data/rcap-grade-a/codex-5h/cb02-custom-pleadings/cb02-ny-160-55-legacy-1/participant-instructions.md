# Participant instructions

Do not file this candidate fixture. Confirm every checklist item and obtain the locally required cover sheet. Stop and seek professional help if any of these applies:
- the district attorney opposes sealing in the interests of justice
- a prior order directed that sealing not occur
- the court disputes whether the September 1980 to October 1991 motion is the correct procedural vehicle
- the court requires a different successor-court or transferred-record procedure
- identity, offense, disposition or conviction date is disputed
- the potential § 160.55(4) exclusion is implicated
- the participant seeks sealing of the actual court file rather than the law-enforcement, DCJS and prosecutor records covered by § 160.55
- an evidentiary hearing, factual investigation or appellate proceeding is required
