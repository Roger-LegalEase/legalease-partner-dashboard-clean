# Arizona application to set aside a conviction

This packet contains the current statewide Form 31(a), Form 31(b), and, when there are more than four counts, a Form 31(a) continuation. It implements the operative text adopted by Arizona Supreme Court Order R-26-0001, effective August 27, 2026.

A set aside is not sealing or expungement. The record remains public with a set-aside annotation.

## Before filing

- **What sentence did the court impose?:** complete this case-specific blank before filing.
- **What is the class of each conviction listed in Section I?:** complete this case-specific blank before filing.
- **Have all probation or sentence conditions been fulfilled, have you been discharged, and on what date?:** complete this case-specific blank before filing.

## Four answers LegalEase must collect and you must put on Form 31(a)

- **What sentence did the court impose?** Form 31(a) Section V, item 1 (Other Information for the Court), or a clearly labeled attached continuation if the answer does not fit.
- **What is the class of each conviction listed in Section I?** Add the class beside its matching conviction in Form 31(a) Section I; use the Section I count continuation immediately after Form 31(a) when needed.
- **Have all probation or sentence conditions been fulfilled, have you been discharged, and on what date?** Answer the printed Form 31(a) Section II compliance choices and put the completion/discharge date in Form 31(a) Section V, item 1, or on a clearly labeled attached continuation.
- **Do you request a certificate of second chance with this application?** State Yes or No in Form 31(a) Section V, item 1, or on a clearly labeled attached continuation. Do not mark any Form 31(b) certificate finding or grant/deny box.

These are participant and case facts. LegalEase must show each one as **Not provided**, **Provided — review and transfer**, or **Invalid**. It must not infer an offense class, a sentence, a completion or discharge date, or a certificate request from the route name. If an answer goes on an attached continuation, label the answer, place the continuation immediately after Form 31(a), tick the Section V attachment box, and list that continuation. Do not put these answers into the proposed order.

Complete every applicable participant choice in Form 31(a): who is applying; sentence compliance and any required explanation; ADOC certificate status; victim restitution; other court-ordered money; prior applications, grants, felony set asides, certificates, and denials; open criminal cases and any explanation; attachments; and whether you request a hearing. These are your case-specific answers. LegalEase does not choose them.

- **Sentence imposed:** describe what the court imposed; a compliance Yes/No answer does not replace the sentence itself.
- **Offense class for every count:** provide the actual class for each conviction; do not infer it from the offense name or statute.
- **Completion and discharge:** give the distinct completion/discharge answer and date; the judgment date is not a substitute.
- **Certificate of second chance request:** answer Yes or No as your requested relief in Form 31(a). This does not decide whether the court will grant it.
- **Certificate of absolute discharge from ADOC:** if you were in Arizona Department of Corrections custody, obtain the certificate and check your sentence-compliance answer against it. Do not mark it present unless you have it.
- **Signature under penalty of perjury — Application, declaration block:** review and sign Form 31(a). No notarization is required.
- **Authorization block — Application, authorization section:** complete it only if an attorney or probation officer applies for the defendant.
- **Fee:** none under A.R.S. § 13-905(B); no fee-waiver form is needed.

File in the court where the person was convicted. Use one application per case number. Submit Form 31(a), any continuation, and the unmarked proposed Form 31(b). Under Arizona Rule of Criminal Procedure 29.2(c), the court sends a copy of the filed application to the applicable prosecuting agency within 10 days after filing. Do not claim that notice has already occurred, and follow any case-specific court directions.

The court owns every finding, grant/deny choice, prosecutor-receipt finding, firearm-right determination, certificate-of-second-chance determination, date, and judicial-officer line in Form 31(b). Do not mark them. A participant's Yes answer requesting a certificate belongs in Form 31(a) and never selects the court's certificate grant or denial.

The defendant, prosecutor, or victim may request a hearing, but the court is not required to set one. The state or victim may object within 30 days after filing.

The current application warns: “I understand that even if I am restored the right to possess and carry a firearm pursuant to this application, I may still be prohibited from possessing and carrying a firearm under other state or federal laws.” The order also warns: “Even if your right to possess and carry a firearm is restored under A.R.S. § 13-905(O), you may still be prohibited from possessing a firearm under other state or federal laws or based on other convictions.” The packet makes no firearm-right eligibility determination.

Stop self-help and seek legal assistance if there is a state or victim objection; the court sets a hearing; victim restitution is unpaid or disputed; the participant reports that sentence conditions are not fulfilled or discharge has not occurred; there is a dangerous-offense, registration, sexual-motivation, or victim-under-fifteen issue; a serious-offense issue may control firearm restoration; the application is denied and you want to challenge it; or you want sealing and do not understand how set-aside relief differs. A routine hearing alone does not stop packet generation, but opposition, disputed evidence, or a contested hearing requires handoff.
