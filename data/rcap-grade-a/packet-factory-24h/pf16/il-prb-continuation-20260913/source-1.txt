    4.  For at least one case, I was arrested and released, and no charges were filed against me.

          program; AND
           a.  I have successfully completed this program; OR
           b.  I am anticipating the successful completion of this program within 61 days of filing this request
                   to expunge.
         In 6, check the box if you have participated in a problem-solving court, pre-plea diversion, or post-plea

         dismissed, the court will review your request to expunge it.

    7.  For at least one case, I was charged, but was later acquitted or the case was dismissed.
         In 7, check the box if you were charged but: you were found not guilty; or the charges were dropped and

         (SOL), non-suit, or dismissal); or the court issued a finding of no probable cause.
         Note: these terms are defined on page 19 of the instructions: ilcourts.info/how-to-expunge-adult

    8.  For at least one case, I received a sentence of Qualified Probation and at least 5 years have passed since
