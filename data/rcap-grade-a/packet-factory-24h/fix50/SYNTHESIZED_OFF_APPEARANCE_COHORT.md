## What this is not

A widget that ships its own appearance for its current state. That stream is the court's own; RI-OFF-APPEARANCE settles that it stays, and the opt-in option does not touch it.

A cohort is a measurement. It authorizes no rebuild, no route change and no promotion, and no family listed here is repaired by being listed.
