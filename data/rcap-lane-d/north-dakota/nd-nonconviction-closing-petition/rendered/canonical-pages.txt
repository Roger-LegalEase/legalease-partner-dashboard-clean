HOW TO FILE THIS PACKET

North Dakota Petition to Close Nonconviction Records
Case: 08-2019-CR-00742
Court: District Court, Burleigh County, North Dakota
County: Burleigh County
Judicial district: South Central Judicial District
Order of nonconviction entered: April 12, 2021

WHY THIS PACKET AND NOT AUTOMATIC CLOSING

The order of nonconviction in this case was entered before August 1, 2025.
  Court records for orders of nonconviction entered on or after August 1, 2025
  close on their own 61 days after the order and need no filing. Because this
  order came earlier, the court record is closed by filing the Petition to
  Close Nonconviction Records in this packet.

WHAT IS IN THIS PACKET

1. Petition to Close Nonconviction Records
2. [Proposed] Order Closing Nonconviction Records
3. Proof of Service on the Prosecuting Attorney (file only if the judge
  requires service)

WHERE TO FILE

File with: Clerk of District Court, Burleigh County, South Central Judicial
  District

Nonconviction records use § 12-60.1-05: orders entered on or after August 1,
  2025 close automatically after 61 days, while earlier orders use a Petition
  to Close Nonconviction Records filed with the clerk of court, with no filing
  fee, where the judge may require service on the prosecutor.

FILING FEE

Nonconviction closing under § 12-60.1-05 (Petition to Close Nonconviction
  Records, for pre-August 1, 2025 orders) has no filing fee.

No filing fee may be charged on a Petition to Close Nonconviction Records, so
  no fee waiver application is part of this packet.

COPIES AND SERVICE

File the petition and the proposed order with the clerk of court, and keep a
  copy of everything filed. If the judge requires service on the prosecuting

  proof of service.

AFTER YOU FILE

Nonconviction closing under § 12-60.1-05: for orders entered before August 1,
  2025, on a Petition to Close Nonconviction Records the court must enter the
  closing order within 10 days if eligible, and no filing fee may be charged.

The source for this route states no hearing requirement and no objection
  period for a Petition to Close Nonconviction Records; it states only that
  the court must enter the closing order within 10 days if the requirements
  are met. No hearing date, objection window, or appearance instruction is
  asserted.

WHAT THE ORDER DOES AND DOES NOT REACH

The North Dakota petition form says the order closes only records controlled
  by the North Dakota court system; it does not close records controlled by
  the prosecutor or law-enforcement entities.

Access to closed nonconviction court records is limited to the clerk, judge,
  juvenile commission, criminal justice agency, defendant, defendant's lawyer,
  state's attorney, or a person with a written judge's order.

IMPORTANT

This North Dakota RCAP draft is legal information, not legal advice, and does
  not guarantee eligibility or any court, agency, or pardon outcome. North
  Dakota is a sealing-first state; relief depends on the exact court record,
  charge, outcome, sentence completion, restitution, new-conviction history,
  and which agency holds the record.
