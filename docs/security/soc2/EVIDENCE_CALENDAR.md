# Evidence calendar

Recurring control actions by cadence. Faith Walls maintains this calendar,
assigns tasks, and escalates missed deadlines to Roger Roman. Legal and privacy
items escalate to Lawrence Blackmon.

A cadence entry is not evidence that the control has ever run.

## Continuous

Owner-scoped data access (IAM-08) · partner tenant isolation (IAM-09) ·
server-authoritative evaluation (PIN-01) · atomic claim (PIN-02) · participant
ownership of records (PIN-05) · privacy-safe telemetry (LOG-06) · central log
collection (LOG-01) · dependency, secret and code scanning (VUL-01/02/03) ·
secret storage (CRY-04) · environment separation (CFG-03) · unique named accounts
(IAM-01) · privileged MFA (IAM-02).

## Per change

Pull-request review (SDLC-03) · required status checks (SDLC-04) · migration
review (SDLC-08) · automated verifier evidence (SDLC-14) · deployment
traceability · rollback plan recorded.

## Per hire, mover or termination

Onboarding checklist (HR-02) · offboarding with same-day revocation (HR-03) ·
joiner/mover/leaver access change (IAM-05) · device assignment and return
(END-01) · policy acknowledgment (TRN-07) · initial training (TRN-01).

## Monthly

Alert review and tuning (LOG-09) · vulnerability finding triage against SLA
(VUL-04) · open exception review (GOV-06) · render-job failure review (AVA-04).

## Quarterly

Privileged access review across every system in AST-01 (IAM-04) · service-account
review (AST-06) · system and service inventory refresh (AST-01) · vendor
inventory refresh (VEN-01) · risk register review (RSK-03) · internal-admin
authorization review (IAM-11).

## Semiannual

Data inventory and flow map refresh (DAT-01) · configuration baseline and drift
review (CFG-03) · capacity review (AVA-04).

## Annual

Risk assessment (RSK-01) · policy review and re-approval (GOV-02) · data
classification review (DAT-02) · retention schedule review (PRI-12) ·
critical-vendor reassessment (VEN-04) · DPA and subprocessor review (VEN-07) ·
security-awareness and role-based training (TRN-01, TRN-04) · incident-response
tabletop (IR-10) · restoration exercise (BCDR-10) · penetration test (VUL-08) ·
cyber-insurance review (RSK-08) · privacy notice review (PRI-01) · management
review of the control program (GOV-01).

## Per incident

Incident record and severity · evidence preservation · legal notification
determination by Lawrence (IR-09) · customer and partner communication ·
post-incident review with remediation tracking.

## Per vendor onboarding or renewal

Risk tiering (VEN-01) · due diligence and security materials (VEN-04) · contract
and DPA review by Lawrence (VEN-07) · vendor access review · termination and data
deletion.

## Per privacy request

Intake and identity verification (PRI-07) · scope determination · retention and
legal-hold review by Lawrence (PRI-12, PRI-13) · execution · processor
propagation · completion receipt and evidence.

## Before observation-period start

Every CCG-C recurring control evidenced at least once · CCG-B production
protection minimum met · CCG-D privacy lifecycle ready · Secureframe mapping
complete with tasks active · every material exception carrying a treatment plan ·
compliance language approved by Lawrence · Faith's evidence-completeness
attestation · Lawrence's legal and privacy approval · Roger's go decision.
