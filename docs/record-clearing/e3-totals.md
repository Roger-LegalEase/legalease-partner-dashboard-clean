## Read this first

**258 mapped pathways is not 262 mapped registry tracks.** They are different denominators counted from different sides. 258 of 324 compiled pathways carry a registry relationship; 262 of 497 registry tracks have a runtime representation. Neither number is a coverage percentage of the other, and quoting the pathway figure against the 497-track denominator overstates coverage substantially.

The registry denominator is **497** and is not altered by anything in this report.
