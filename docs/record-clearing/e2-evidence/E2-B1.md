## Why these 64 rows were unresolved, and why most now map

The automated crosswalk matcher only compared statutory citations and form identifiers appearing
in the compiled pathway **id or label** against registry track authorities. Most of these pathways
carry their operative citations in their **rule clauses, summaries, official-form source text, or
embedded official statute URLs** — places the matcher never looked. 53 of the 64 jobs close as
`mapped_to_registry_track` on subsection-level statutory citations, shared official form
identifiers, or state-pack citations; 10 close as genuine `registry_gap`; 1 closes as
`registry_scoped_out_named_authority` on the registry’s own recorded statement.

Typical matcher misses this lane repaired: zero-padded citations (HI `§ 291E-0064(e)` vs the
track’s `§ 291E-64(e)`), citations living in embedded official URLs (FL `0943.0581`, ME
`title15sec3308-C`, LA `legis.la.gov` article pages), citations in official-form source text (the
KSJC petition, the KY AOC-JV-29 order), and label citations the tokenizer failed to parse
(DE `11-del-c-4374`, LA `art-998`, MA `100c`).
