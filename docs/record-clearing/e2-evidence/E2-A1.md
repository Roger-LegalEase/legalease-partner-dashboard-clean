## Conventions used

- **Discriminators.** Every resolution rests on an operative statutory citation (including
  subsection containment, e.g. § 15-27-2(a)(6) ∈ § 15-27-2(a)), an official form identifier
  (JD-CR-202, AOCCREM1I-071221, JDF 417, ACIC uniform petition-and-order, CR41FORM31A/B), or an
  explicit scope restriction stated in the compiled source. Name similarity was never used.
- **Shared-corpus bleed.** Several profiles (AL most severely) append a shared agent-training corpus
  to every pathway, so raw citation *presence* does not discriminate. Only pathway-defining
  labels/summaries/leading clauses were treated as evidence; this is noted per finding.
- **Terminal = missing_from_compiled_runtime.** Where the sole candidate was affirmatively refuted
  (wrong subsection, petition-vs-automatic mode mismatch, disposition scope) and a profile-wide
  search proved the track's operative authority appears nowhere, the job is closed terminal with an
  `absence_proof` recording exactly what was searched and its hit count.
- **Mappings outside the candidate pool.** For 10 jobs the discriminating evidence points at a
  compiled pathway the frozen candidate pool omitted (e.g. AL trafficking → the trafficking pathway,
  CT clean-slate tracks → the two clean-slate pathways, KS specialty/prostitution/registration →
  their dedicated pathways, HI DAG/DANC → the deferred-acceptance pathway). Nothing was invented:
  each such mapping carries the statutory/form quote from the target pathway plus the refutation of
  the listed candidate. E3 adjudicates.
