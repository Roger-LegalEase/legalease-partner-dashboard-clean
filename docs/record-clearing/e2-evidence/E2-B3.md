## Why this whole group existed

The crosswalk generator matched on pathway ids and labels only. These compiled profiles carry their statutory citations inside `summary`, `ruleClauses`, and `orderedDecisionRules` — not in ids or labels — so every pathway fell out as `unresolved_no_candidate` even where the operative citation match is exact (e.g., PA path-j quotes 18 Pa.C.S. 9122.2 verbatim; the registry track pa_9122_2_clean_slate is built on the same section). 48 of 63 jobs closed by extracting those in-text citations and joining them to registry track authority arrays.
