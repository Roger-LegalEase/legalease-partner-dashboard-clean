# MVLP Clinic Mode walkthrough: six-minute script and shot list

Record at 1440×900 for staff screens and on a phone (or a 390-px browser window) for participant screens. Use only synthetic names; the training clinic is fine. Say screen labels exactly as they appear.

| Time | Shot | On screen | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | 1. Title over the MVLP page | `/p/mvlp` (phone) | "This is how an MVLP expungement clinic runs in Clinic Mode: registration, a private application, MVLP's review, documents, and follow-up. Everything the participant does starts on the MVLP page." |
| 0:25–1:05 | 2. Register | `/p/mvlp/clinics` → **Register for this clinic** → **Sign in or create your account** → registration form → **We have your registration** | "The participant picks a clinic and creates a free account. Registration asks only for a name, a way to reach them, and any help they need at the clinic. It holds a place; it is not yet MVLP's decision." |
| 1:05–2:05 | 3. Application | **Start my application**; scroll steps 1–10; type an answer and show the **Saved** badge; **Protected information** step | "The application follows MVLP's intake. Answers save on the server as you go, so people can stop and come back at *Continue my application*. Money questions have *I don't know this amount*, which is never treated as zero. The Social Security number has its own protected step: it is stored encrypted, shown masked, and only the attorney or coordinator can reveal it, with the reason recorded." |
| 2:05–2:40 | 4. Sign and submit | **Review and sign**: read a statement, **Sign this statement** (draw one), **Submit my application**, **Your application has been received** | "Only the applicant signs. Each signature is tied to the exact wording and the answers on screen; change an answer later and you sign again. Then submit." |
| 2:40–3:30 | 5. Staff review | `/clinic/staff/<event id>/applications` → applicant → **Start review** → **Send request** → **Record decision** | "Staff sign in with their own accounts and see only the clinics they are assigned to. The coordinator starts the review, can ask the applicant for something missing, and the program reviewer records MVLP's decision with a reason. The financial summary shows what counts; food benefits are listed as not counted, and without an approved income table it always says a person decides." |
| 3:30–4:20 | 6. Attorney and documents | **Take attorney assignment**, **Reveal once** (purpose), **Add a court document** with the prepared packet, **open prepared packet**, **Mark attorney reviewed** → **Mark signature or notary pending** | "The attorney takes the assignment, reviews the matter, and attaches the participant's prepared packet as the unsigned copy. Each document moves through review, execution and filing in order. The system never adds a seal or a signature." |
| 4:20–4:55 | 7. Notary | Notary account → **Notarization** → **Upload the signed copy** → **Record executed copy received** | "The notary sees only what needs notarizing, not the intake. After notarizing on paper, they upload the signed copy and record that it was received; the attorney then marks it reviewed, ready to file, and filed." |
| 4:55–5:30 | 8. Follow-up and the participant's view | **Participant next steps** → **Add next step**; **Contact the participant** → **Send and record**; switch to phone: `/p/mvlp/continue` | "Next steps are assigned to an owner and appear on the participant's own page in plain English. Messages report what really happened: sent, or recorded but not sent." |
| 5:30–6:00 | 9. Export and close | **Case-file export** → **Export case file** → PDF; event page **Close event** | "When MVLP needs its case file, the export is recorded and the protected number stays masked unless the attorney chooses otherwise. Close the event at the end; staff keep access to the cases they own. Questions: 601-960-9577 or mvlp@mvlp.org." |

Do not show a real applicant, a real Social Security number, a staff password, or an invitation link. Cut the sixty-second reveal short in editing.
