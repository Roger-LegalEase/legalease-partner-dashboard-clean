### Virginia: distinguish mechanism before mapping a form

For an October 12, 2026 evaluation, the official page's version effective UNTIL December 1, 2026 controls, not its separately printed future version. Section 19.2-392.2(A)/(C) concerns the ordinary acquittal/dismissal petition. Subsection (I), together with 2.2-402, addresses court implementation of an absolute pardon for a crime the person did not commit. These are not interchangeable.

The official catalog now publishes CC-1473, revision 07/26, for the A-subsection petition. Visible elements include DOB, name at arrest, charge/disposition information and separate signer contact fields, including email. An email line is not proof of an eligibility requirement. Do not automatically substitute this form on `va_exp_absolute_pardon` or declare CC-1472 retired without an official basis. Reconcile the mechanism, exact template and applicable edition first. Signature, clerk, hearing and third-party acts remain unexecuted unless genuinely performed. The private companion CSV supplies semantic ownership, not invented coordinates or AcroForm IDs.

### Delaware: resolve the four disputed exclusion rows by mechanism

11 Del. C. 4373(b) excludes specified offenses from MANDATORY expungement. Section 4374(a)(2) expressly addresses discretionary expungement of misdemeanors listed in 4373(b), subject to its seven-year/later-of-conviction-or-release and criminal-history conditions. Section 4374(b) supplies separate discretionary exclusions. General 4372(f) and pardon 4375 rules remain distinct. A mandatory-track exclusion is therefore not a universal Delaware denial, nor proof that the discretionary track succeeds. Bind the exact rows to their mechanism; never union every track's exclusions into a pre-route denial list.
