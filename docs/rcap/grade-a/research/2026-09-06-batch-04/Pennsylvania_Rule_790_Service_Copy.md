# Pennsylvania Rule 790: service wording for the existing certificate

Date: September 6, 2026. Family: `pa_790_nonconviction-set`.
Proposed completion text for the existing certificate, not a newly accepted official form.

Rule 790's Comment directs filing/service to Rule 576 and treats this petition as a motion for Rules 575–577. The paper-service method is therefore not an unanswerable local-law question. Current recipient addresses and any e-filing choice remain local inputs.

## Certificate content

Use the original case caption and docket number. A proposed body is:

> I certify that on [actual service date] I served a copy of [identify the filed document] on the persons/offices listed below by the method recorded for each recipient.

| Recipient and capacity | Address used | Telephone number | Actual service method |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Attorney for the Commonwealth] | [Verified recipient address] | [Verified number] | [Method actually used] |
| [Court administrator] | [Verified recipient address] | [Verified number] | [Method actually used] |
| [Other party, if applicable] | [Verified recipient address] | [Verified number] | [Method actually used] |

Signature of unrepresented petitioner: ____________________

Do not pre-certify service. Identify all actual parties; do not add fictional recipients or numbers. Mailing to the Commonwealth's attorney is an available method; service upon the court administrator has its own listed methods. For electronic filing, follow Rule 576.1 rather than assume an emailed document accomplishes service.

See PA1 and PA2 in Source_Index.json. Preserve court-approved formatting and any currently applicable privacy requirements. The certificate itself does not establish that any service has taken place.
