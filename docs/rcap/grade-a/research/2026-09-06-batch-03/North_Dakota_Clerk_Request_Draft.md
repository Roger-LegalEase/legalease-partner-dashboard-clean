## Conditions before this text may be generated

Confirm the entered date and qualifying whole-case disposition; apply the statutory exclusions, including whether the case was appealed; calculate expiration correctly; confirm continued **court-system** public access. A missing or disputed fact must not be invented. A private background-check listing alone does not satisfy the court-record predicate.

Record the actual sent/received dates separately. The proposed five-business-day administrative follow-up interval runs from confirmed receipt only if the product adopts that rule. It is not stated here as a statutory requirement. Express refusal or a court instruction can trigger the approved next step without that additional wait.

A later enforcement motion is a different document. Its legal adoption and Rule 3.2 notice/brief, Rule 47 hearing notice, and Rule 49 service cannot be replaced by sending this letter. Keep the original letter and response as evidence; do not prefill a certificate of service for an act that has not occurred.
