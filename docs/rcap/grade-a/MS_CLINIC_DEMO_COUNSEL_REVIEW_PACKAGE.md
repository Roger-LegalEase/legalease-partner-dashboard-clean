## Participant instructions

- Compare the court, filing location, cause number, and legal name against the
  certified disposition and docket sheet.
- Confirm an actual arrest and release. Copy the exact charge, citation,
  classification, dates, arrest name, aliases, agency identifier, and
  disposition wording from the records rather than memory.
- Insert the certified disposition as Exhibit A and the docket sheet as Exhibit
  B. The generated checklist is not an attachment.
- Do not sign in advance. Sign the petition and verification in a notary's
  physical presence after taking an oath or affirmation; the notary completes
  the certificate and stamp.
- Use the court-approved MCIC identifier channel. Remove the confidential MCIC
  addendum from participant and service copies.
- Confirm the prosecuting office, current service address, timing, and any
  approval-as-to-form practice with the court of origin.
- Ask the court of origin about its filing method, fee or waiver, and required
  number of copies.
- Leave participant signature and service-time fields blank until those acts
  occur, and leave prosecutor, clerk, judge, notary, and seal fields for their
  owners.

## Filing destination

File in the court that handled the case. Before filing, contact that court's
records office to confirm whether the petition uses the existing cause number
or a new filing number and whether the court requires a preferred local form or
filing method. The intended office is the records office or clerk for the
participant-confirmed court in the participant-confirmed filing location.

## Fee treatment

The packet states no universal fee amount. The participant must ask the records
office of the court that handled the case whether a fee applies to this
subsection (4) petition. If a fee applies and the participant cannot afford it,
the participant must ask that court which pauper's affidavit or fee-waiver
process it accepts. The packet does not assume a statewide amount or waiver
form.

## Service treatment

The participant provides a public service copy to the confirmed prosecuting
authority only after the court or prosecutor confirms the service address and
local timing. The confidential MCIC identifier addendum must not be included.
The certificate leaves method, date, and signature blank until service occurs.
The participant must ask the court whether advance notice, approval as to form,
or another local step is required; the packet does not import the felony
ten-day notice rule into this route.

## Every self-help stop

Stop and obtain qualified legal help when any of these conditions exists:

1. The docket says "passed to the file," "retired to the file," remanded, or
   uses another ambiguous case-ending entry.
2. The case moved between courts or the court of origin is unclear.
3. The case involved nonadjudication, pretrial intervention, diversion,
   intervention court, or drug court.
4. A related charge or co-defendant matter remains pending.
5. The prosecuting authority refuses approval as to form where local practice
   expects it; the participant may not sign for the prosecutor or represent
   that approval was given.
6. The participant is not a United States citizen or has any immigration
   concern.
