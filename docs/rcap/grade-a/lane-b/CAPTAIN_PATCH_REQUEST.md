## 3. `data/rcap-grade-a/**` — the v1 → v2 record migration

**This is the substantive one, and nothing sells until it happens.**

Every record in the shipped registry declares
`rcap-grade-a-fulfillment-authority/v1`. v1 records are evaluated exactly as
before — the projection is unchanged and `generate --check` stays green — but
**admission refuses every one of them**, whatever its evaluated state, because a
record written before fileability was a question cannot answer it.

So the current position is: wiring Lane F today closes every commercial door.
That is correct and fail-closed. Re-opening any of them is this migration.

### What a v2 record adds

One block, `packetCompleteness`, carrying a versioned and hashed specification
identity plus eleven fields: the nine Grade-A filing dimensions
(`filingApplication`, `proposedOrder`, `attachmentsAndSchedules`,
`serviceAndNotice`, `filingDestination`, `feeAndWaiverInstructions`,
`copyRequirements`, `postFilingSteps`, `hearingAndObjectionStopConditions`), each
`{ state, basis }`; `customPleadingAuthority`; and `filingFormatArtifact` with
its own format, SHA-256 and page count.

`not_required` is a real answer for eight of the nine, but it is a legal
conclusion, so it carries the basis that reached it. `filingApplication` may
never be waived.

### A worked example is committed

`data/rcap-lane-b/v2-candidate-record.json`, regenerated by
`scripts/generate-rcap-grade-a-lane-b-v2-candidate.mjs`. It is **not** a registry
entry and says so in its own document. It is a transcription target, so the
migration is not a design exercise.

It covers `ND:first-offense-possession-sealing`, the only route in the registry
with a formal packet specification — and that specification covers all nine
dimensions, so its fileability proof is real and cited section by section.

### What the worked example changed, and why

| Field | v1 | v2 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `packetSpecification.specId` | `nd-marijuana-first-offense-seal-set` | `nd-first-offense-possession-sealing` | v1 binds the packet **set** id and a launch-graph-derived hash. The formal specification is a different, later document with its own id, version and hash, and it is the one carrying the filing sections. Binding it is the substantive part. |
| `officialSources` | `[]` | 3 entries, 1 held | v1 was empty because the launch graph reported no official **form** ids — and this route needs none (`officialPdfRequired: false`; ch. 19-03.1 prescribes no form). Its real sources are the owner-approved packet-set manifest, which is held and hashable, and two compiled-profile sections which are not. |
| `filingFormatArtifact` | — | pdf, `97d288c0…`, 7 pages | Real committed bytes. The base gate renders this packet, pins the hash, and proved determinism across processes after finding pdf-lib was stamping the wall clock into it. |
| `customPleadingAuthority` | — | approved, `auth-2026-08-19-…` | **The one judgement call, flagged for your review.** The owner-approved packet-set manifest is treated as authority to *draft* a composed pleading. That is not counsel approving the *output*, which stays pending as its own dimension. If you disagree, set `approved: false` and the route simply stays closed on one more gap. |

### What it still does not fix

The worked v2 record is `INCOMPLETE` with four real gaps, and no amount of
record-writing closes them:

1. **`ND-profile:section:90-95` and `ND-profile:corpus-unit:38` are not held.**
   Their verification is `asserted_by_ingestion`; `private/` is absent from this
   container. Lane C and Lane D reverify against the corpus — Lane B must not,
   and did not, accept an asserted identity as a verified one.
2. **Output legal approval is `not_performed`.** Counsel's, per the packet
   record's own hold reason.
3. **Visual review is `not_performed_no_rasteriser_in_this_runtime`.** Page
   images, not page structure. Needs a runtime that can rasterise.
4. **Final verification is unbound.** No verification has been run and bound to
   this exact proof set.

The other seven registry routes have no formal packet specification at all
(`data/record-clearing/packet-specifications/` holds exactly one file), so they
cannot be migrated to v2 until one exists for each.

### Ordering

Do **not** regenerate `data/rcap-grade-a/*.json` from
`scripts/generate-rcap-grade-a-fulfillment-authority.mjs` expecting v2 records:
that generator derives from the launch graph and emits v1. Teaching it v2 means
teaching it to read packet specifications, which is a change to a generator this
lane does not own. Either extend it, or transcribe from the worked example.

## 4. `scripts/generate-rcap-launch-graph.mjs` — one authority, not two

Standing request, carried forward. `operationallySellable` is a tenth
independent computation of commercial eligibility, and its nine operational gates
do not include page-by-page visual review, output-level legal approval, provider
identity, fixture binding or final verification. It should become:

```js
fulfillmentAuthorityFor(row.pathwayKey).authorized
```

with the existing gates kept as diagnostics. Until then the launch graph and the
runtime can disagree, and the launch graph is the more permissive of the two.
